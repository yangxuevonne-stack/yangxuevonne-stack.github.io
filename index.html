使命Mission

这个网站，是为那些正在或即将踏上美国并购与创业之路的公司、企业家与创业者而建。希望把一线积累下来的判断与经验，转化成可以直接参考的实战建议——让每一次关键决策，都少走一段弯路，多一分底气。 This space is built for the companies, entrepreneurs, and founders charting their path through US M&A and entrepreneurship. The hope is to turn frontline experience into practical guidance you can actually use — so every critical decision is made with fewer blind spots, and a little more confidence.
