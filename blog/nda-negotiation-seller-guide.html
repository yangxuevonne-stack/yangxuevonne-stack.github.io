M&A卖方NDA谈判完全指南：13个关键条款逐一拆解 Seller's Complete Guide to M&A NDA Negotiation: 13 Key Clauses Decoded

April 2026 · 18 min read

在并购交易中，NDA（保密协议）往往被视为流程性文件，卖方律师随手签完就进入下一阶段。这是一个危险的误区。

NDA不只是保密承诺，它是整场并购谈判的第一道防线——也常常是卖方最后一次以平等地位坐上谈判桌的机会。一旦买方完成尽职调查，掌握了你的客户名单、技术架构、财务预测和核心员工信息，谈判砝码就已向买方倾斜。

本文从卖方视角，将NDA的13个核心条款按风险等级逐一拆解，提供实战谈判策略和建议条款语言，帮助你在签署第一份文件时就把保护锁定到位。

In M&A transactions, NDAs are often treated as procedural documents — quickly signed and forgotten before moving to the next stage. This is a dangerous misconception.

An NDA is not merely a confidentiality promise. It is the first line of defense in the entire deal — and often the last time a seller negotiates from a position of relative equality. Once the buyer completes due diligence and gains access to your customer lists, technical architecture, financial projections, and key employee data, leverage shifts decisively toward the buyer.

This guide analyzes 13 core NDA clauses from the seller's perspective, rated by risk level, with practical negotiation strategies and model clause language to help you lock in protection from the first document you sign.

机密信息的定义：你披露的到底是什么？Definition of Confidential Information: What Are You Actually Disclosing?

机密信息的定义是整个NDA的基础——定义越宽，买方能获取和保留的信息就越多；定义越模糊，争议就越多。买方律师通常会把定义写得极为宽泛，涵盖"任何形式的所有信息"，而卖方往往没有意识到这意味着什么。

⚠ 卖方常见陷阱
  • 接受"任何形式、任何渠道披露的所有信息"的宽泛定义
  • 未明确排除公开信息、买方独立开发的信息
  • 未要求书面标注"机密"才构成受保护信息（口头信息也应有后续书面确认义务）
  • 允许定义延伸至卖方员工、客户、供应商的个人信息
✓ 谈判策略
  • 将定义限定为"仅与本次潜在交易直接相关的信息"
  • 明确列出排除项：公开信息、买方在披露前已掌握的信息、买方独立开发的信息、第三方合法提供的信息
  • 要求口头披露须在72小时内以书面确认方可受保护
  • 对特别敏感的信息（如核心算法、客户名单）单独建立更高保护等级

建议条款语言

The definition of confidential information is the foundation of the entire NDA. The broader the definition, the more information the buyer can access and retain. Buyer counsel typically drafts this to cover "all information in any form," and sellers often fail to appreciate the implications.

⚠ Common Seller Traps
  • Accepting overbroad definitions covering "all information in any form or medium"
  • Failing to carve out publicly available information and independently developed information
  • Not requiring written marking as "Confidential" to trigger protection
  • Allowing the definition to extend to personal data of employees, customers, or suppliers
✓ Negotiation Strategy
  • Limit the definition to "information directly related to the potential transaction"
  • Enumerate exclusions: public information, information known before disclosure, independently developed information, and third-party-sourced information
  • Require oral disclosures to be confirmed in writing within 72 hours to be protected
  • Establish heightened protection tiers for especially sensitive information (e.g., core algorithms, customer lists)

Model Clause Language

"Confidential Information" means only that information disclosed by the
Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party that (i) is directly related to
the Potential Transaction, (ii) if disclosed in tangible form, is marked
as "Confidential" at the time of disclosure, or (iii) if disclosed orally,
is identified as confidential at the time of disclosure and confirmed in
writing within seventy-two (72) hours of such disclosure.

Confidential Information shall NOT include information that:
(a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the
    Receiving Party;
(b) was rightfully known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure;
(c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference
    to the Confidential Information; or
(d) is received from a third party without restriction on disclosure.

极高风险Critical Risk

停滞条款（Standstill）：卖方最危险的让步Standstill Clause: The Seller's Most Dangerous Concession

停滞条款限制卖方在特定期间寻找其他潜在买家或公开出售意向。这条条款直接影响卖方获得最优估值的能力，是NDA中卖方最需要警惕的条款之一。

⚠ 卖方常见陷阱
  • 接受无明确截止日期的开放式停滞义务
  • 未保留fiduciary out（受托人例外），在收到更优报价时无法自由响应
  • 停滞期超过90天，实质上让买方完成尽调后再决定是否继续
  • 未约定买方违约时停滞义务自动终止
✓ 谈判策略
  • 拒绝在NDA层面设置停滞条款，将其推后至LOI（意向书）阶段谈判
  • 若无法拒绝，设置不超过30-45天的硬性日落条款
  • 明确保留fiduciary out：董事会有义务响应更优要约时，停滞义务自动中止
  • 约定买方提前终止谈判时，停滞义务立即终止

建议条款语言

A standstill clause restricts the seller from seeking other potential buyers or publicizing sale intentions during a specified period. This clause directly undermines the seller's ability to achieve optimal valuation and is one of the most dangerous provisions a seller can agree to in an NDA.

⚠ Common Seller Traps
  • Accepting open-ended standstill obligations with no defined end date
  • Failing to preserve a fiduciary out when a superior offer emerges
  • Standstill periods exceeding 90 days, effectively letting the buyer complete diligence before deciding
  • No automatic termination of standstill upon buyer breach
✓ Negotiation Strategy
  • Refuse standstill provisions at the NDA stage; defer them to the LOI negotiation
  • If unavoidable, insist on a hard sunset of no more than 30–45 days
  • Explicitly preserve a fiduciary out: standstill suspends automatically when the board is obligated to respond to a superior proposal
  • Specify that the standstill terminates immediately upon buyer's early termination of negotiations

Model Clause Language

For a period not to exceed forty-five (45) days from the date hereof
(the "Standstill Period"), the Company agrees not to solicit acquisition
proposals from third parties; provided, however, that nothing herein
shall prevent the Company's Board of Directors from:

(i)  responding to any unsolicited bona fide acquisition proposal from a
     third party, to the extent required by its fiduciary duties;
(ii) engaging in discussions or negotiations in response to such a
     proposal; or
(iii) taking any action the Board determines in good faith is required
      by its fiduciary duties.

This standstill shall terminate automatically upon: (a) the expiration
of the Standstill Period; (b) Buyer's written notice of intent to cease
negotiations; or (c) Buyer's material breach of this Agreement.

极高风险Critical Risk

竞业禁止与禁止招揽：交易失败后的枷锁Non-Compete & Non-Solicit: Shackles That Survive Deal Failure

买方律师有时会将竞业禁止或禁止招揽条款嵌入NDA，而非等到交割后再谈。这意味着即便交易失败，卖方创始人或核心团队也可能在数年内无法在同行业创业或工作，或无法接触自己的前客户。

⚠ 卖方常见陷阱
  • 在NDA阶段就同意竞业禁止，且未限定触发条件为"交割完成"
  • 禁止招揽范围过宽，涵盖所有现有及潜在客户
  • 禁止员工招揽期限超过2年
  • 未约定交易未完成时所有限制性义务自动终止
✓ 谈判策略
  • 坚持：NDA阶段不谈竞业禁止，该义务应在Purchase Agreement中作为交割条件谈判
  • 若买方坚持，加入明确的终止条款：交易未于[X]日期前完成，所有限制自动终止
  • 将禁止招揽限定为"在尽职调查过程中接触到的员工"，而非全体员工
  • 要求对应的对价：若卖方在NDA阶段接受限制性义务，买方应给予书面承诺或补偿

建议条款语言

Buyer counsel sometimes embeds non-compete or non-solicit provisions in the NDA rather than saving them for post-closing. This means that even if the deal fails, the seller's founders or key team members may be unable to operate in the same industry or contact their own former customers for years.

⚠ Common Seller Traps
  • Agreeing to non-compete restrictions at NDA stage without limiting the trigger to "Closing"
  • Non-solicit scope that is overly broad, covering all existing and prospective customers
  • Employee non-solicit periods exceeding two years
  • No automatic termination of all restrictive covenants if the deal fails
✓ Negotiation Strategy
  • Insist: no non-compete at the NDA stage; reserve for Purchase Agreement as a closing condition
  • If buyer insists, add a clear termination provision: all restrictions terminate automatically if the transaction does not close by [Date]
  • Limit non-solicit to "employees contacted during due diligence," not the entire workforce
  • Demand reciprocal consideration: if seller accepts restrictive covenants at NDA stage, buyer must provide written commitment or compensation

Model Clause Language

Any non-competition or non-solicitation obligations contained herein
are expressly conditioned upon and shall only become effective upon
the consummation of the Transaction (the "Closing").

In the event the Transaction does not close for any reason on or
before [DATE], all restrictive covenants set forth in this Agreement,
including any non-competition, non-solicitation, and no-hire provisions,
shall automatically terminate and be of no further force or effect,
without any further action by either party.

Any employee non-solicitation obligation shall be limited solely to
those employees of the Company with whom Buyer had direct contact
during the due diligence process.

高风险High Risk

允许的披露范围：买方可以分享给谁？Permitted Disclosures: Who Can the Buyer Tell?

买方需要与律师、财务顾问、融资银行等讨论交易。问题在于：NDA是否对这些"允许接收人"设置了足够的约束？如果买方的"顾问"包括竞争对手或潜在竞标方，这将是灾难性的。

⚠ 卖方常见陷阱
  • 允许披露给"代理人和顾问"，但未限定范围或要求签署保密承诺
  • 未要求买方对其顾问的违约行为承担连带责任
  • 允许向融资方披露，但未限定融资方为有资质的机构投资人
  • 未禁止将信息披露给买方的竞争对手或战略合作伙伴
✓ 谈判策略
  • 限定允许接收人为"需要知悉以评估交易的人员"（need-to-know basis）
  • 要求买方让其顾问签署同等保密承诺，或在NDA中直接约束顾问
  • 明确买方对其顾问的违约行为承担直接责任
  • 禁止向买方的现有或潜在竞争对手披露任何信息

建议条款语言

Buyers need to discuss the transaction with lawyers, financial advisors, and financing banks. The critical question is whether the NDA imposes adequate restrictions on these "permitted recipients." If the buyer's "advisors" include competitors or rival bidders, the consequences could be catastrophic.

⚠ Common Seller Traps
  • Permitting disclosure to "agents and advisors" without scope limits or confidentiality commitment requirements
  • Failing to hold the buyer vicariously liable for its advisors' breaches
  • Allowing disclosure to financing parties without limiting them to qualified institutional investors
  • Not prohibiting disclosure to buyer's competitors or strategic partners
✓ Negotiation Strategy
  • Limit permitted recipients to those who "need to know for purposes of evaluating the Transaction"
  • Require buyer to have its advisors execute equivalent confidentiality commitments
  • Hold buyer directly liable for any breach by its permitted recipients
  • Expressly prohibit disclosure to buyer's existing or prospective competitors

Model Clause Language

Buyer may disclose Confidential Information only to those of its
directors, officers, employees, legal counsel, financial advisors, and
financing sources who (i) have a need to know such information solely
for the purpose of evaluating the Potential Transaction, (ii) are
informed of the confidential nature of the information, and (iii) are
bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those
set forth herein (each, a "Permitted Recipient").

Buyer shall be responsible for any breach of this Agreement by any
Permitted Recipient as if such breach were committed by Buyer directly.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, Buyer shall not disclose any
Confidential Information to any Person who is, to Buyer's knowledge,
a competitor of the Company or an affiliate of a competitor.

高风险High Risk

残留信息条款（Residual Clause）：最难察觉的漏洞Residual Clause: The Hardest Loophole to Spot

残留信息条款允许买方员工将"记忆中保留的"机密信息用于其他目的，因为无法证明这些信息来自卖方的披露。这条条款直接摧毁了NDA对技术秘密、商业策略等软性知识产权的保护效力。

⚠ 卖方常见陷阱
  • 未注意到协议中隐藏的残留信息条款（通常措辞为"personnel who retain Confidential Information in unaided memory"）
  • 接受残留信息条款适用于所有机密信息，而非仅限于一般性商业信息
  • 未意识到该条款实质上允许买方的工程师团队"记住"产品架构和技术方案后另行使用
✓ 谈判策略
  • 首选：完全删除残留信息条款
  • 若买方坚持，明确排除以下内容不适用残留条款：商业秘密、客户名单、定价信息、技术架构
  • 加入限制：残留信息的使用不得用于与卖方竞争，或在卖方对应产品/服务的市场中使用
  • 要求买方对因残留信息使用导致的损失承担证明责任

建议条款语言

The residual clause permits buyer employees to use confidential information "retained in unaided memory" for other purposes, on the theory that such use cannot be proven to derive from the seller's disclosure. This provision directly undermines NDA protection for trade secrets, commercial strategies, and other intangible intellectual property.

⚠ Common Seller Traps
  • Missing a residual clause buried in the agreement (typically phrased as "personnel who retain Confidential Information in unaided memory")
  • Allowing the residual clause to apply to all confidential information rather than general business information only
  • Failing to recognize that this effectively permits buyer's engineers to "remember" product architecture and technical solutions for independent use
✓ Negotiation Strategy
  • First preference: delete the residual clause entirely
  • If buyer insists, expressly exclude from the residual carve-out: trade secrets, customer lists, pricing data, and technical architecture
  • Add restriction: residual information may not be used to compete with the Company or in markets where the Company offers corresponding products or services
  • Require buyer to bear the burden of proof for losses caused by use of residual information

Model Clause Language

Notwithstanding anything to the contrary herein, no Residual
Information exception shall apply to any Confidential Information
constituting:
(i)   trade secrets of the Company under applicable law;
(ii)  customer lists, customer contact information, or pricing data;
(iii) technical architecture, source code, or proprietary algorithms; or
(iv)  any other information that the Company has designated in writing
      as subject to heightened protection.

Any permitted use of Residual Information shall not be used, directly
or indirectly, to compete with the Company or to develop products or
services competitive with those offered by the Company.

高风险High Risk

保密期限与存续期：时间就是保护Term & Survival Period: Time Is Protection

买方通常希望将保密义务期限压短——1-2年的保密期对于包含技术秘密和商业战略的信息远远不够。而且保密期届满后，买方就可以合法使用和披露这些信息。

⚠ 卖方常见陷阱
  • 接受1-2年的保密期，技术信息尚未被市场消化，保护就已失效
  • 未区分一般商业信息与商业秘密的不同存续期
  • 未约定交易终止/NDA终止后的存续期
✓ 谈判策略
  • 一般商业信息：要求至少3-5年保密期
  • 商业秘密：要求永久保密，或与该信息保持商业秘密地位同等期限
  • 明确约定：NDA终止后，已披露信息的保密义务继续存续

建议条款语言

Buyers typically seek to shorten confidentiality periods. A 1–2 year term is wholly inadequate for information containing trade secrets and commercial strategy. Upon expiration, the buyer is free to use and disclose the information.

⚠ Common Seller Traps
  • Accepting a 1–2 year term for technical information that markets haven't yet absorbed
  • Failing to distinguish survival periods for general business information versus trade secrets
  • No specified survival period following termination of the transaction or the NDA
✓ Negotiation Strategy
  • General business information: require at least 3–5 years
  • Trade secrets: require permanent confidentiality, or a duration co-extensive with trade secret status under applicable law
  • Expressly provide that confidentiality obligations survive termination of the NDA with respect to information already disclosed

Model Clause Language

The obligations of the Receiving Party under this Agreement shall
survive for a period of five (5) years from the date of disclosure
of the applicable Confidential Information, except that obligations
with respect to any Confidential Information constituting a trade
secret under applicable law shall continue for so long as such
information remains a trade secret, and in any event shall not
terminate less than ten (10) years from the date of this Agreement.

All confidentiality obligations under this Agreement shall survive
the expiration or earlier termination of this Agreement with respect
to Confidential Information disclosed prior to such expiration
or termination.

中风险Medium Risk

救济措施：违约了你能做什么？Remedies: What Can You Do When Breach Occurs?

NDA被违反后，卖方最重要的救济是禁令救济（injunctive relief）——阻止买方继续使用或披露信息。如果NDA中没有明确约定可以寻求禁令救济，买方律师会争辩说金钱赔偿已经足够，这会让卖方陷入旷日持久的损害赔偿诉讼。

⚠ 卖方常见陷阱
  • 救济条款中仅提到金钱赔偿，未明确约定禁令救济
  • 未约定违约方放弃对禁令救济申请提出担保要求的抗辩
  • 未明确损害赔偿的最低金额或清算损害赔偿条款
✓ 谈判策略
  • 明确约定：卖方有权寻求specific performance和injunctive relief，无需证明金钱赔偿不足
  • 约定买方放弃要求卖方提供担保的权利（waiver of bond requirement）
  • 考虑加入律师费转移条款：违约方承担胜诉方的律师费

建议条款语言

After an NDA breach, the seller's most critical remedy is injunctive relief — stopping the buyer from continuing to use or disclose the information. Without an express provision allowing the seller to seek injunctive relief, buyer's counsel will argue that monetary damages are adequate, trapping the seller in protracted damages litigation.

⚠ Common Seller Traps
  • Remedy provisions limited to monetary damages with no mention of injunctive relief
  • No agreement by the breaching party to waive the requirement for the non-breaching party to post a bond
  • No liquidated damages provision or minimum damages floor
✓ Negotiation Strategy
  • Expressly provide that the seller is entitled to seek specific performance and injunctive relief without the need to prove that monetary damages are inadequate
  • Include a bond waiver: buyer waives any requirement that seller post a security bond
  • Consider a fee-shifting provision: the breaching party bears the prevailing party's attorneys' fees

Model Clause Language

Each party acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement would cause
irreparable injury to the Disclosing Party for which monetary damages
would be an inadequate remedy. Accordingly, the Disclosing Party shall
be entitled, without the necessity of proving actual damages or posting
any bond or other security, to seek equitable relief, including
injunction and specific performance, in addition to all other remedies
available at law or in equity.

The Receiving Party hereby waives any defense that: (i) an adequate
remedy at law exists; or (ii) equitable relief is inappropriate in the
circumstances.

In any action to enforce this Agreement, the prevailing party shall
be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs from
the non-prevailing party.
8
中风险Medium Risk

信息归还与销毁：交易失败后你的信息去哪里？Return & Destruction: Where Does Your Information Go After Deal Failure?

交易失败后，卖方的机密信息仍存在于买方的服务器、邮件、分析报告和顾问手中。如果没有严格的归还/销毁条款，这些信息可能被用于买方日后对卖方的竞争。

⚠ 卖方常见陷阱
  • 仅要求"尽合理努力"归还或销毁，而非强制性义务
  • 允许买方保留"法律要求保留"的副本，但未限定其用途
  • 无书面确认销毁义务，卖方无法验证信息是否已被清除
  • 归还/销毁义务不适用于买方顾问持有的副本
✓ 谈判策略
  • 约定在卖方要求或交易终止后10个工作日内，必须完成归还或销毁
  • 要求买方在销毁完成后提供书面确认证明
  • 保留的法律合规副本应隔离保存，且仅可用于合规目的
  • 销毁义务明确延伸至买方所有顾问和允许接收人

建议条款语言

After deal failure, the seller's confidential information remains on buyer's servers, in emails, analyst reports, and with advisors. Without strict return/destruction provisions, that information can be used for future competition against the seller.

⚠ Common Seller Traps
  • Only "commercially reasonable efforts" to return or destroy, rather than a mandatory obligation
  • Allowing buyer to retain copies "required by law" without restricting their use
  • No written confirmation of destruction, leaving the seller unable to verify compliance
  • Return/destruction obligations that do not extend to copies held by buyer's advisors
✓ Negotiation Strategy
  • Require return or destruction within 10 business days of the seller's written request or termination of discussions
  • Require a written certificate of destruction upon completion
  • Copies retained for legal compliance must be segregated and used solely for compliance purposes
  • Destruction obligation must expressly extend to all advisors and permitted recipients

Model Clause Language

Promptly upon the written request of the Disclosing Party or upon
the termination of discussions regarding the Potential Transaction,
but in no event later than ten (10) business days thereafter,
Receiving Party shall, and shall cause each of its Permitted Recipients
to, at Disclosing Party's election, either:
(a) return all Confidential Information and all copies thereof; or
(b) certify in writing the permanent deletion and destruction of
    all Confidential Information and all copies thereof.

Receiving Party shall provide a written officer's certificate
confirming compliance with this Section within five (5) business days
of completing such return or destruction.

Receiving Party may retain copies solely to the extent required by
applicable law or regulation; provided that such retained copies shall
be maintained in a segregated repository, shall not be accessed except
as required by law, and shall be deleted as soon as legally permitted.
9
中风险Medium Risk

排他性条款（Exclusivity）：谁限制了谁？Exclusivity: Who Is Really Constrained?

排他性条款要求卖方在特定期间内不与其他潜在买家谈判。这与停滞条款有所不同——停滞针对公开市场行为，排他性针对私下谈判。排他性本质上是卖方送给买方的免费选择权。

⚠ 卖方常见陷阱
  • 在NDA阶段就同意排他性，而非在LOI阶段以对价换取
  • 排他期超过30天，且未附加买方的进展义务
  • 排他期届满后未约定自动失效，买方可主张期限顺延
✓ 谈判策略
  • 拒绝在NDA阶段给予排他性，将其作为LOI谈判筹码
  • 若必须给予，将排他期压缩至最多30天，且须附加买方的具体进展里程碑
  • 设置买方"合理进展"义务：未按计划推进尽调，排他期自动终止
  • 要求对等：排他期内买方也不得与其他卖方谈判同类交易

建议条款语言

An exclusivity clause requires the seller not to negotiate with other potential buyers during a specified period. Unlike a standstill (which addresses public market behavior), exclusivity targets private negotiations. Exclusivity is, in essence, a free option granted by the seller to the buyer.

⚠ Common Seller Traps
  • Granting exclusivity at the NDA stage rather than extracting consideration at the LOI stage
  • Exclusivity periods exceeding 30 days without buyer progress milestones
  • No automatic expiration upon the end of the exclusivity period
✓ Negotiation Strategy
  • Refuse exclusivity at the NDA stage; reserve it as LOI negotiation leverage
  • If unavoidable, cap the exclusivity period at 30 days and attach concrete buyer progress milestones
  • Include a "reasonable progress" obligation: failure to advance due diligence on schedule terminates exclusivity automatically
  • Require reciprocity: during exclusivity, buyer also may not negotiate comparable transactions with other sellers

Model Clause Language

Any exclusivity granted hereunder shall be limited to thirty (30) days
from the date hereof (the "Exclusivity Period") and shall terminate
automatically at the end of such period without any further action.

Exclusivity shall terminate prior to the end of the Exclusivity Period
if:
(a) Buyer fails to provide a written indication of interest setting
    forth proposed material terms within fifteen (15) days hereof;
(b) Buyer materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such
    breach within five (5) business days of written notice; or
(c) Buyer notifies the Company in writing that it no longer intends
    to pursue the Potential Transaction.

During the Exclusivity Period, Buyer shall devote commercially
reasonable efforts to advance due diligence and transaction
documentation in good faith.

较低风险Lower Risk

管辖法律与争议解决：在哪里打官司？Governing Law & Dispute Resolution: Where Do You Fight?

管辖法律和争议解决条款决定了一旦发生争议，将在哪个司法管辖区、以何种方式解决。对于在美国进行并购的中国企业卖方，这一条款尤为重要。

⚠ 卖方常见陷阱
  • 接受对卖方不利的管辖地（如买方所在州），增加诉讼成本
  • 未约定争议解决方式（诉讼 vs 仲裁），导致程序不确定性
  • 选择对商业秘密保护不利的州法律
✓ 谈判策略
  • 优先选择对商业秘密保护完善的州：特拉华州或加利福尼亚州
  • 考虑约定JAMS仲裁，加速争议解决，降低诉讼成本
  • 明确约定双方同意在选定管辖地的个人管辖权

建议条款语言

Governing law and dispute resolution provisions determine where and how disputes will be resolved. For Chinese enterprise sellers conducting M&A in the United States, this clause carries particular significance.

⚠ Common Seller Traps
  • Accepting an unfavorable forum (e.g., buyer's home state), increasing litigation costs
  • No specification of dispute resolution mechanism (litigation vs. arbitration), creating procedural uncertainty
  • Choosing a state law unfavorable to trade secret protection
✓ Negotiation Strategy
  • Prefer states with robust trade secret protection: Delaware or California
  • Consider JAMS arbitration to accelerate dispute resolution and reduce litigation costs
  • Expressly provide for each party's consent to personal jurisdiction in the selected forum

Model Clause Language

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with
the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-
laws provisions.

Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be
resolved by binding arbitration administered by JAMS pursuant to
its Comprehensive Arbitration Rules and Procedures, before a single
arbitrator with experience in commercial transactions. The seat of
arbitration shall be [City, State]. Judgment on the award rendered
may be entered in any court of competent jurisdiction.

Each party irrevocably consents to the personal jurisdiction of the
state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware for the
purpose of enforcing any arbitral award hereunder or for interim
equitable relief pending arbitration.

公告限制：确保双向约束Announcement Restrictions: Ensure Mutual Binding

公告限制条款规定双方不得未经对方同意就交易事宜进行公开声明。对于上市公司买方，信息披露规则可能与NDA条款产生冲突，需要特别处理。

✓ 谈判策略
  • 确保公告限制为双向约束，而非仅限制卖方
  • 如买方为上市公司，约定在法定披露前须给予卖方合理通知时间（至少48小时）
  • 约定任何公告内容须经双方书面同意

建议条款语言

Announcement restriction clauses prevent either party from making public statements about the transaction without the other's consent. For publicly traded buyers, disclosure rules may conflict with NDA provisions and require special handling.

✓ Negotiation Strategy
  • Ensure announcement restrictions are mutual, not limited to the seller
  • If buyer is publicly traded, require reasonable advance notice to seller before any required disclosure (at least 48 hours)
  • Require written consent of both parties for the content of any announcement

Model Clause Language

Neither party shall, without the prior written consent of the other
party, make any public announcement or statement regarding the
Potential Transaction or the existence of this Agreement.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, if either party is required by
applicable law, regulation, or stock exchange rule to make a public
disclosure, such party shall: (i) provide the other party with no
less than forty-eight (48) hours' prior written notice; (ii) consult
in good faith with the other party regarding the form and content of
the disclosure; and (iii) disclose only that information which is
legally required to be disclosed.

知识产权保护：披露不等于转让IP Protection: Disclosure Is Not Assignment

尽职调查中难免需要披露专利、商业秘密、软件代码等知识产权信息。必须明确这些披露不构成任何知识产权权利的转让或许可。

✓ 谈判策略
  • 明确所有知识产权归卖方所有，披露不构成任何形式的许可或转让
  • 禁止买方对卖方任何专利提出无效挑战（no-challenge clause）
  • 约定买方不得申请与卖方披露信息实质相同或相似的专利

建议条款语言

Due diligence inevitably requires disclosure of patents, trade secrets, and source code. It is critical to establish that these disclosures do not constitute any assignment or license of intellectual property rights.

✓ Negotiation Strategy
  • Expressly confirm that all IP remains owned by the seller and disclosure constitutes no license or assignment in any form
  • Include a no-challenge clause prohibiting buyer from challenging the validity of any seller patent
  • Prohibit buyer from filing for patents substantially similar to or based on the seller's disclosed information

Model Clause Language

Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting the
Receiving Party any right, title, interest, or license in or to any
Confidential Information or any intellectual property rights of the
Disclosing Party, by implication, estoppel, or otherwise.

All Confidential Information remains the sole and exclusive property
of the Disclosing Party. The Receiving Party agrees not to:
(i)  challenge the validity or enforceability of any of Disclosing
     Party's intellectual property rights; or
(ii) file any patent application that claims priority to or is
     substantially based on any Confidential Information disclosed
     hereunder.

不可抗力与一般条款：魔鬼藏在细节里Force Majeure & Boilerplate: The Devil Is in the Details

不可抗力条款和其他"一般条款"（整体协议、可分割性、弃权等）虽然风险相对较低，但措辞不当仍可能带来麻烦。特别是在网络安全事件频发的今天，不可抗力条款可能被买方用于免除数据泄露后的保密义务。

✓ 谈判策略
  • 明确不可抗力条款不适用于数据安全事件或网络攻击
  • 确保整体协议条款（entire agreement）不覆盖此前双方签署的其他保密安排
  • 可分割性条款应确保任一条款无效不影响其他条款效力
  • 弃权须以书面形式作出，且不构成对后续权利的弃权

建议条款语言

Force majeure and other "boilerplate" clauses (entire agreement, severability, waiver, etc.) carry relatively lower risk, but improper drafting can still create problems. In an era of frequent cybersecurity incidents, force majeure provisions may be invoked by buyers to excuse post-breach confidentiality obligations.

✓ Negotiation Strategy
  • Expressly exclude data security incidents and cyber attacks from the force majeure carve-out
  • Ensure the entire agreement clause does not supersede prior confidentiality arrangements between the parties
  • Severability should confirm that invalidity of any provision does not affect the remainder
  • Waivers must be in writing and shall not constitute waiver of subsequent rights

Model Clause Language

For purposes of this Agreement, "Force Majeure" means events beyond
a party's reasonable control, including natural disasters, war, or
governmental action; provided, however, that Force Majeure shall not
include: (i) any cybersecurity breach, data security incident, or
unauthorized access to information systems; or (ii) any financial
hardship or economic downturn affecting either party.

No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement shall
operate as a waiver of such right. Any waiver must be in writing and
signed by the waiving party and shall not be construed as a waiver of
any subsequent or other right.

If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or
unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force
and effect. Any invalid provision shall be modified to the minimum
extent necessary to make it valid and enforceable.
