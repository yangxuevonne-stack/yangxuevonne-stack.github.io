Seller's M&A NDA Playbook: Eight Clauses That Define Your Protection

文章摘要Summary
在并购交易启动之前，买卖双方通常先签署保密协议（NDA）。对卖方而言，这远不是一道形式关——其条款直接决定商业秘密、客户信息和谈判筹码能否得到切实保护。本文从卖方角度深度拆解8个最关键的谈判条款，每个条款附英文示范语言及买卖双方谈判立场对比，帮助您在尽调开始前锁定最有利的保护条件。 Before any M&A process formally begins, buyers and sellers sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement. For sellers, this document is far more than a formality — its terms directly determine whether your trade secrets, customer data, and negotiating leverage receive real protection. This guide dissects eight critical clauses from the seller's perspective, with sample language and buy-side vs. sell-side position comparisons, so you can secure maximum protection before due diligence begins.

Why the NDA Is Critical for Sellers
为什么NDA对卖方如此重要

并购交易的第一道门是保密协议。卖方在向潜在买方开放数据室之前，必须确保NDA的每一个条款都能真正保护你的核心利益。一旦签署，商业秘密、客户名单、财务模型、定价策略就将暴露在对方面前——如果NDA存在漏洞，这些信息可能被用于竞争、谈判施压，甚至在交易破裂后仍流通于市场。

本文从卖方律师视角，梳理8个最值得逐条谈判的核心条款，并为每个条款提供：风险评级、谈判目标、买卖双方立场对比，以及可直接引用的英文示范语言。

The NDA is the first gate of any M&A process. Before opening a data room to potential buyers, sellers must ensure every clause genuinely protects their core interests. Once signed, trade secrets, customer lists, financial models, and pricing strategies become accessible to the counterparty — if the NDA has gaps, this information can be weaponized in negotiations, used competitively, or continue circulating in the market even after deal failure.

This guide reviews eight clauses most worth negotiating line by line, with risk ratings, negotiation objectives, buy-side vs. sell-side position comparisons, and sample language ready for direct use.

⚠ 实践提示⚠ Practice Note

NDA谈判往往被双方低估。买方倾向于使用其标准模板，卖方若不主动提出修改，极有可能在保护关键信息、限制买方行为等方面处于不利地位。

NDA negotiations are frequently underestimated by both sides. Buyers typically push their standard-form templates; sellers who do not proactively negotiate terms are likely to end up with inadequate protection for key information and insufficient behavioral restrictions on the buyer.

条款一Clause 1

机密信息的定义范围高风险

Definition of Confidential InformationHigh Risk

这是整份NDA的基石条款。定义越宽泛，卖方保护越充分。买方倾向于缩窄定义（如"仅书面标注'机密'的材料"），而卖方应坚持将所有在尽调期间披露的非公开信息纳入保护范围，无论以何种形式传递。

卖方谈判目标：涵盖口头、书面、电子形式的一切非公开信息；明确包含财务数据、客户名单、定价策略、技术文档及内部沟通记录；排除条款应严格限定（仅限已公开信息、买方独立开发信息等）。

  • 不以书面标注"机密"为前提条件
  • 明确将口头披露信息纳入范围，要求买方在披露后48小时内以书面确认
  • 排除条款中"独立开发"须有举证责任倒置条款
  • 特别保护：客户名单、定价模型、并购谈判策略本身

This is the foundation of the entire NDA. The broader the definition, the stronger the seller's protection. Buyers typically seek to narrow the scope (e.g., "only materials marked 'Confidential'"), while sellers should insist that all non-public information disclosed during due diligence falls within scope, regardless of form.

Seller's Negotiation Objective: Cover all non-public information in oral, written, or electronic form; explicitly include financial data, customer lists, pricing strategies, technical documentation, and internal communications; limit exclusions strictly.

  • No requirement that materials be marked "Confidential"
  • Oral disclosures expressly included, with written confirmation within 48 hours
  • "Independently developed" exclusion subject to reverse burden of proof
  • Special protection for customer lists, pricing models, and deal negotiation strategy itself

买方立场Buyer 仅书面标注"机密"的材料受保护，口头信息须另行书面确认 Only written materials marked "Confidential"; oral disclosures require separate written confirmation

Sample Clause — Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by Seller to Recipient in connection with the Transaction, whether disclosed orally, in writing, electronically, or by any other means, including without limitation financial statements, customer and supplier lists, pricing data, technical specifications, business strategies, and the terms of the Transaction itself, regardless of whether such information is marked or identified as confidential. For oral disclosures, Seller shall provide written confirmation within 48 hours; failure to confirm shall not exclude such information from protection hereunder.
条款二Clause 2

停滞条款（Standstill）高风险

Standstill ProvisionHigh Risk

停滞条款禁止买方在保密协议有效期内，未经卖方董事会同意，直接向卖方股东发起收购要约或积累卖方股票。对于上市公司卖方这是保护控制权的核心防线；对于私有公司卖方，停滞条款同样可限制买方单方面提升报价压力或绕过正式流程。

关键谈判点：期限通常为12–18个月；应加入"Don't-Ask, Don't-Waive"（DADW）条款，防止买方私下要求豁免停滞限制；禁止买方向卖方股东或管理层单独游说。

  • 明确限制买方积累卖方股票（无论通过一级市场还是二级市场）
  • 禁止发起不请自来的要约（unsolicited offer）
  • DADW条款：买方不得主动请求卖方豁免停滞条款
  • 限制期结束后，应规定一个"静默期"后方可行动

The standstill prevents the buyer from acquiring seller's securities, launching a tender offer to seller's shareholders, or accumulating a position in seller's stock without board approval during the NDA's term. For public company sellers, this is the core defense of board control; for private sellers, it prevents buyers from unilaterally escalating pressure or bypassing the formal process.

Key Negotiation Points: Term is typically 12–18 months; a "Don't-Ask, Don't-Waive" (DADW) clause prevents the buyer from privately requesting a standstill waiver; prohibit individual lobbying of seller's shareholders or management.

  • Expressly restrict buyer from accumulating seller's stock (primary or secondary market)
  • Prohibit unsolicited offers directly to shareholders
  • DADW clause: buyer may not request seller to waive the standstill
  • Post-expiry "quiet period" before buyer may take action

谈判立场对比Negotiation Position Comparison
卖方立场Seller 18个月限制，含DADW条款，禁止任何形式股权积累 18-month restriction with DADW; prohibit all forms of equity accumulation
买方立场Buyer 要求删除DADW条款，期限缩短至6个月，或要求例外情形更宽泛 Remove DADW, shorten to 6 months, or seek broader carve-outs
市场惯例Market Standard 12–18个月，DADW在战略收购中逐渐成为标准条款 12–18 months; DADW increasingly standard in strategic M&A
Sample Clause — Standstill
Recipient agrees that, for a period of 18 months from the date hereof, it will not, directly or indirectly, without the prior written consent of Seller's Board of Directors: (i) acquire or seek to acquire beneficial ownership of any securities, assets, or businesses of Seller; (ii) make or participate in any "solicitation" of proxies to vote any securities of Seller; (iii) seek to influence or control the management, board, or policies of Seller; (iv) enter into any discussions, negotiations, or agreements with any third party regarding any of the foregoing; or (v) request that Seller or its Board of Directors waive, amend, or grant any exception to the provisions of this paragraph (a "Don't-Ask, Don't-Waive" obligation).
条款三Clause 3

竞业禁止与不挖角条款中风险

Non-Compete & Non-SolicitationMedium Risk

双向不挖角条款是市场标准实践，但卖方应确保条款真正对等：买方不得在谈判期间及之后一定期限内，直接或间接招募卖方的关键员工、高管或技术人员，无论是否通过正式招聘渠道。

竞业禁止条款（Non-Compete）较少出现在NDA层面（通常在SPA中约定），但如买方提出，卖方需警惕范围和期限是否过于宽泛，影响未来经营自主权。

卖方谈判要点：限制期建议12–24个月；明确"solicitation"的触发标准（区分主动接触与回应公开招聘）；"关键员工"须在附件中列明；任何基于NDA获取的信息不得用于招聘决策。

Mutual non-solicitation is market standard, but sellers should ensure genuine reciprocity: the buyer must not, directly or indirectly, recruit the seller's key employees, executives, or technical staff during negotiations and for a defined period afterward, whether through formal channels or otherwise.

Non-compete provisions rarely appear at the NDA stage (typically addressed in the SPA), but if a buyer raises them, sellers must watch for overly broad scope or duration that could impair future operational autonomy.

Seller's Key Points: Restrict for 12–24 months; define what constitutes "solicitation" (distinguish active outreach from responding to public postings); "Key Employees" should be listed in an exhibit; information obtained via NDA may not be used in hiring decisions.

谈判立场对比Negotiation Position Comparison
卖方立场Seller 24个月双向不挖角，关键员工附件列明，主动接触即构成违约 24-month mutual non-solicit; key employees listed in exhibit; any active outreach = breach
买方立场Buyer 12个月，排除HR部门主动发出的一般性职位广告 12 months; exclude general job postings by HR department
市场惯例Market Standard 12–18个月，一般广告豁免，但定向接触明确禁止 12–18 months; general advertising exempt; targeted outreach prohibited
Sample Clause — Non-Solicitation
For a period of 24 months following the date hereof, each Party agrees not to, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment, hire, or retain any Key Employee of the other Party who was introduced to or identified by such Party in connection with the Transaction. "Key Employee" means the individuals listed on Exhibit A hereto, as updated by mutual written agreement. General advertisements or postings not targeted at such individuals shall not constitute solicitation, provided that responding to such a posting by a Key Employee who has been specifically targeted shall not be deemed a general advertisement.
条款四Clause 4

允许的披露情形中风险

Permitted Disclosure Carve-OutsMedium Risk

买方通常需要将机密信息分享给其律师、财务顾问、投资银行、会计师等（统称"代表"）以完成尽职调查。卖方的谈判目标是：最小化接收方范围、要求买方对其代表的违约行为承担连带主责任，并禁止任何超出评估交易目的之外的信息使用。

关键条款：need-to-know原则；买方代表须签署独立保密协议或受同等约束；买方承担连带责任（而非仅承担监督责任）；明确禁止将信息用于其他并购机会。

  • 接收方须在"need-to-know"基础上限定
  • 买方应就代表的违约行为承担主要责任（primary liability），不得仅承担补充责任
  • 法定信息披露（如监管要求）须提前通知卖方，并配合卖方申请保护令
  • 信息不得用于评估本次交易以外的任何目的，包括竞争分析或其他潜在并购目标

Buyers typically need to share confidential information with counsel, financial advisors, investment banks, and accountants (collectively "Representatives") to complete due diligence. The seller's objective is to minimize the recipient pool, require the buyer to take primary liability for any breach by its representatives, and prohibit any use of information beyond evaluating the transaction.

Key Terms: Need-to-know basis; buyer's representatives must sign separate NDAs or be bound by equivalent obligations; buyer takes primary liability (not merely supervisory); information may not be used for any other M&A opportunity.

  • Recipients limited on a strict need-to-know basis
  • Buyer takes primary liability for representative breaches — not merely secondary
  • Compelled disclosures (regulatory, court order) require advance notice and cooperation with protective orders
  • Information may not be used for competitive analysis or evaluation of other acquisition targets
Sample Clause — Permitted Disclosure
Recipient may disclose Confidential Information solely to its Representatives who have a bona fide need to know such information for the sole purpose of evaluating the Transaction, provided that: (a) such Representatives are informed of the confidential nature of the information and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less restrictive than those set forth herein; (b) Recipient shall be primarily liable for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives, as if such breach were Recipient's own; and (c) Recipient shall not, and shall cause its Representatives not to, use any Confidential Information for any purpose other than evaluating the Transaction, including without limitation for competitive intelligence or the evaluation of any other potential acquisition or investment.
条款五Clause 5

期限与存续义务中风险

Term, Survival & Destruction ObligationMedium Risk

保密义务的存续期建议不少于3年。商业秘密类信息（技术秘诀、核心算法、客户定价模型）应单独约定永久保密义务。谈判失败后，卖方应要求买方在10个工作日内销毁或归还全部机密材料，并提供书面销毁确认函。

注意事项：IT系统的自动备份通常被豁免，但仍应受保密约束；销毁确认函须由买方授权高级别代表签署；应约定买方有权就销毁行为核查审计。

  • 一般信息：保密期3年；商业秘密：永久
  • 交易失败或终止后10个工作日内销毁/归还并书面确认
  • 自动备份豁免但仍受协议约束，不得被主动检索或使用
  • 卖方有权要求提供合规证明文件

Confidentiality obligations should survive for no less than three years. Trade secret information (technical know-how, core algorithms, customer pricing models) should carry a perpetual obligation in a separate sub-clause. Following a failed process, sellers should require the buyer to destroy or return all confidential materials within 10 business days and provide a written certification of destruction.

Key Considerations: Automated IT backups are typically carved out but must remain subject to confidentiality obligations; destruction certificate should be signed by a senior authorized representative; seller should have audit rights to verify compliance.

  • General information: 3-year survival; trade secrets: perpetual
  • Destruction/return within 10 business days of deal failure, with written certification
  • Auto-backups exempt from destruction but not from confidentiality — may not be actively retrieved or used
  • Seller retains right to request compliance certification at any time

谈判立场对比Negotiation Position Comparison
卖方立场Seller 3年+商业秘密永久保密；10日内销毁并书面确认 3 years + perpetual for trade secrets; destruction within 10 days with written cert
买方立场Buyer 1–2年，无永久条款，销毁义务宽泛化（"商业合理努力"） 1–2 years; no perpetual clause; destruction obligation limited to "commercially reasonable efforts"

Sample Clause — Term, Survival & Destruction
The obligations of confidentiality hereunder shall survive for a period of three (3) years from the date hereof; provided that obligations with respect to information constituting trade secrets under applicable law shall survive indefinitely. Upon the written request of Seller following termination of discussions regarding the Transaction, Recipient shall, within 10 business days, destroy or return all Confidential Information (including all copies and extracts) and deliver written certification of such destruction signed by an authorized senior officer of Recipient, except for archival copies retained solely in automated backup systems, which shall remain subject to this Agreement and may not be actively accessed or used.
条款六Clause 6

违约救济措施高风险

Remedies for BreachHigh Risk

NDA的救济条款决定违约发生后卖方能多快、多有效地获得保护。损害赔偿往往难以量化（商业秘密外泄的实际损失难以证明），因此卖方应明确约定禁令救济权，并要求法院不得要求提供担保金作为禁令前提。

核心条款：（1）禁令救济权（无需证明货币损害已足够）；（2）无需缴纳保证金；（3）律师费转移条款（胜诉方可要求败诉方承担合理律师费）；（4）损害赔偿不设上限（或设置充分高的上限）。

  • 明确约定违约情形下卖方有权申请禁令或特定履行
  • 法院批准禁令时无需要求卖方提供担保金
  • 律师费条款：故意违约情形下败诉方承担全部合理诉讼费用
  • 不得以损害赔偿作为禁令的替代救济

The remedy clause determines how quickly and effectively the seller can obtain protection after a breach. Monetary damages for confidentiality violations are notoriously difficult to quantify (actual losses from trade secret exposure are hard to prove), so sellers must expressly reserve the right to seek injunctive relief without being required to post a bond.

Core Terms: (1) Injunctive relief without proving monetary damages are inadequate; (2) No bond requirement; (3) Fee-shifting (prevailing party may recover reasonable attorneys' fees); (4) No cap on damages (or a sufficiently high cap).

  • Expressly reserve seller's right to seek injunctive relief or specific performance
  • Court may grant injunction without requiring seller to post a security bond
  • Fee-shifting: in cases of willful breach, breaching party bears all reasonable litigation costs
  • Monetary damages shall not be deemed an adequate substitute for injunctive relief
Sample Clause — Remedies
Each Party acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm to the other Party for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy, and that the non-breaching Party shall be entitled to seek specific performance, injunctive relief, or other equitable relief without the requirement of posting any bond or other security. In any action arising out of or relating to a willful breach of this Agreement, the prevailing Party shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs from the non-prevailing Party. The foregoing remedies shall be in addition to, and not in lieu of, any other remedies available at law or in equity.
条款七Clause 7

管辖法律与争议解决低风险

Governing Law & Dispute ResolutionLow Risk

管辖法律的选择看似技术性问题，实则影响保密义务的解释、商业秘密的认定标准以及禁令救济的可获得性。美国并购实践中，特拉华州法律是最常见的选择（尤其对上市公司卖方），因其成熟的公司法体系和禁令裁量标准对卖方相对友好。

卖方谈判目标：选择对卖方有利的管辖法律（通常为卖方注册地或主要业务所在州）；指定有保护卖方案例积累的法院；明确专属管辖权，排除其他司法区的并行诉讼风险。

  • 优先选择卖方熟悉的司法管辖区（如特拉华州）
  • 争议解决机制：通常诉讼优于仲裁（仲裁裁决执行在某些司法区存在不确定性）
  • 如选择仲裁，应明确排除"保全措施"须经仲裁庭批准的条款，确保法院禁令权保留
  • 专属管辖权条款应明确，禁止在其他司法区提起平行诉讼

The choice of governing law is technically nuanced but critically affects how confidentiality obligations are interpreted, trade secrets are defined, and injunctive relief is obtained. In U.S. M&A practice, Delaware law is the most common choice (especially for public company sellers), given its mature corporate law framework and seller-friendly injunctive relief standards.

Seller's Objective: Select a jurisdiction favorable to the seller (typically seller's state of incorporation or principal operations); specify courts with a strong record of protecting sellers; establish exclusive jurisdiction to eliminate parallel litigation risk.

  • Prefer seller's familiar jurisdiction (e.g., Delaware)
  • Litigation generally preferred over arbitration (arbitral award enforcement can be uncertain)
  • If arbitration chosen, ensure courts retain power to grant interim injunctive relief without arbitral panel approval
  • Exclusive jurisdiction clause must be airtight to prevent parallel proceedings elsewhere
Sample Clause — Governing Law & Jurisdiction
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of law principles. Each Party hereby irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if the Court of Chancery lacks jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware) for the resolution of any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement. Each Party waives any objection to the laying of venue of any such proceeding in such courts and waives any objection that such courts are an inconvenient forum. Notwithstanding the foregoing, each Party retains the right to seek emergency interim relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.
条款八Clause 8

知识产权保护中风险

Intellectual Property ProtectionMedium Risk

在科技类、生命科学类并购中，知识产权条款尤为关键。买方在尽调过程中接触到的专利申请、技术秘诀、源代码、算法等，必须明确不因NDA或数据共享产生任何权利转让或许可效果。同时，禁止买方基于卖方披露的信息申请专利或进行"反向工程"。

关键条款：明确NDA不构成任何IP权利的授予；禁止利用披露信息申请知识产权；要求买方的知识产权团队成员单独签署IP相关保密承诺；专利申请对价监控条款。

  • NDA不构成任何专利、版权、商标或商业秘密的许可或转让
  • 禁止基于卖方信息直接或间接申请专利
  • 禁止对卖方产品或技术进行反向工程
  • 买方IP团队须单独签署强化版保密承诺书
  • 违反本条款视为重大违约，触发最高额损害赔偿

In technology and life sciences M&A, IP protection clauses are especially critical. Patents in prosecution, technical know-how, source code, and algorithms accessed during due diligence must be expressly excluded from any transfer or license effect arising from the NDA or data-sharing. Buyers must also be prohibited from filing patents based on seller's disclosures or reverse-engineering seller's products.

Key Terms: NDA does not grant any IP rights; prohibit patent filings based on disclosed information; require separate IP-specific confidentiality undertakings from buyer's IP team members; monitor for suspicious patent activity post-signing.

  • NDA constitutes no license or assignment of any patent, copyright, trademark, or trade secret
  • Buyer prohibited from filing patents directly or indirectly based on seller's disclosed information
  • Reverse engineering of seller's products or technology expressly prohibited
  • Buyer's IP team members must sign enhanced IP-specific confidentiality undertakings
  • Breach of IP clause constitutes material breach triggering maximum damages
Sample Clause — IP Protection
Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to grant Recipient any license, right, title, or interest in or to any intellectual property of Seller, including without limitation any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or know-how disclosed hereunder. Recipient shall not, and shall cause its Representatives not to: (i) file, prosecute, or cause to be filed any patent application that incorporates, is derived from, or is enabled by any Confidential Information; (ii) reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble any product, technology, or process of Seller; or (iii) use any Confidential Information to design around or design alternatives to Seller's intellectual property. Any breach of this Section shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement.
