Abstract
对卖方而言，NDA不仅是程序性文件，更是并购谈判中第一道实质性防线。本文逐条解析买方常用的8大不利条款，帮助卖方在签署前识别并修正风险。 For sellers, an NDA is not merely procedural — it is the first substantive line of defense in M&A. This guide dissects eight buyer-favorable provisions and equips sellers to identify and redline them before signing.

机密信息定义 Confidential Information Definition

Confidential Information Definition Risk Level: HIGH
CLAUSE 01 高风险HIGH RISK

买方草案往往将"机密信息"定义得极为宽泛，甚至涵盖"以任何方式披露的一切信息"，同时附加大量例外——如"已属公知领域""买方独立开发"等。这种不对等设计对卖方极为不利：买方只要声称信息来自独立渠道，就能规避保密义务。

谈判策略：推动以书面形式交付机密信息为原则，口头披露须在48小时内以书面形式确认；同时要求"独立开发"例外附加证明义务，即买方须提供书面证据证明其开发路径独立于卖方信息。

Buyer-drafted NDAs routinely define "Confidential Information" with sweeping breadth — covering "all information disclosed in any manner" — while building in broad exceptions such as "publicly available" or "independently developed." This asymmetry benefits buyers: they can disclaim obligations merely by asserting independent discovery.

Negotiation strategy: Push for written delivery as the default mode; oral disclosures should require written confirmation within 48 hours. Require that the "independent development" carve-out come with a documentation obligation — buyer must maintain written records establishing a development path independent of seller's information.

// Seller-Friendly Redline
"Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by
Seller to Buyer in written or electronic form and marked
"CONFIDENTIAL," or disclosed orally and confirmed in writing
within 48 hours of disclosure. The independent development
exception shall apply only if Buyer can demonstrate, by
contemporaneous written records, that such development occurred
without use or reference to any Confidential Information of Seller.
条款 02Clause 02

停滞条款 Standstill Provision

Standstill Provision Risk Level: HIGH
CLAUSE 02 高风险HIGH RISK

停滞条款（Standstill）限制买方在谈判期间收购卖方股份或发起敌意收购行动。从表面看，这对卖方有利。但许多买方草案在此条款中附加"静默条款"（don't-ask-don't-waive），即禁止买方在董事会未主动邀请的情况下提交更高报价。这实际上剥夺了卖方在谈判陷入僵局时引入竞争性报价的机会。

谈判策略：明确删除"don't-ask-don't-waive"语言；同时将停滞期限设定为明确的天数（如12个月），不得无限期延续；并约定若董事会收到第三方要约，停滞义务自动解除。

A standstill clause restricts the buyer from accumulating seller's shares or launching a hostile bid during negotiations. On its face, this favors the seller. However, many buyer drafts embed a "don't-ask-don't-waive" provision — prohibiting the buyer from even requesting a waiver without board invitation. This effectively prevents sellers from soliciting competing bids when talks stall.

Negotiation strategy: Expressly strike "don't-ask-don't-waive" language. Set a fixed standstill period (e.g., 12 months) and provide that standstill obligations automatically terminate if the board receives a bona fide third-party proposal.

// Seller-Friendly Redline
Standstill Period: 12 months from the date hereof.
Buyer shall not, directly or indirectly, acquire beneficial
ownership of any Shares of Seller during the Standstill Period
without prior written consent of Seller's Board.
NOTWITHSTANDING THE FOREGOING, all standstill obligations shall
automatically terminate upon Seller's Board receiving a written
acquisition proposal from any third party.
条款 03Clause 03

竞业禁止与非招揽 Non-Compete & Non-Solicit

Non-Compete / Non-Solicit Risk Level: HIGH
CLAUSE 03 高风险HIGH RISK

部分NDA在保密条款之外夹带竞业禁止与非招揽条款，限制卖方在谈判破裂后从事相关业务或挖角自身员工。这类条款在谈判过程中往往被忽视，但一旦签署，其法律效力与独立竞业协议相当。

谈判策略：要求将竞业禁止与非招揽条款从NDA中剥离，留待正式收购协议（SPA）再行谈判；若买方坚持，应将地理范围、业务范围和期限严格限定，且仅在交割完成后方能生效。

Some NDAs embed non-compete and non-solicitation obligations alongside confidentiality terms — restricting seller from operating competing businesses or recruiting its own employees after deal failure. These are easy to miss during review but carry the same legal weight as standalone agreements once signed.

Negotiation strategy: Require that non-compete and non-solicit provisions be removed from the NDA and reserved for negotiation in the definitive purchase agreement (SPA). If buyer insists, strictly limit geographic scope, business scope, and duration — and condition effectiveness on closing.

// Seller-Friendly Redline
This Agreement does not contain, and shall not be construed to
contain, any restriction on Seller's right to operate its business,
hire employees, or compete in any market. Any non-competition or
non-solicitation obligations, if agreed, shall be set forth
exclusively in a definitive acquisition agreement and shall be
conditioned upon consummation of the Transaction.
条款 04Clause 04

允许的披露 Permitted Disclosures

Permitted Disclosures Risk Level: MED
CLAUSE 04 中风险MED RISK

买方草案通常允许其将机密信息披露给"代表"（Representatives），但对"代表"的定义宽泛，可能涵盖竞争对手旗下的联属机构或私募基金的多个投资组合公司。卖方需明确限制谁可以接触信息，并要求这些人也受到同等约束。

谈判策略：明确列举"代表"的类别（仅限法律顾问、财务顾问和直接负责评估的内部人员）；要求所有代表签署与NDA同等约束力的书面承诺；并要求买方对代表的违约行为承担连带责任。

Buyer drafts typically permit disclosure to "Representatives" — a term often defined broadly enough to include affiliates controlled by competitors or multiple portfolio companies of a private equity fund. Sellers must constrain who touches the data and ensure those parties are equally bound.

Negotiation strategy: Enumerate "Representatives" by category (limited to legal counsel, financial advisors, and internal personnel directly involved in evaluation). Require all Representatives to execute written undertakings no less restrictive than this Agreement. Make Buyer liable for Representatives' breaches as if they were Buyer's own.

// Seller-Friendly Redline
"Representatives" means solely (i) Buyer's legal counsel,
(ii) Buyer's financial advisors, and (iii) Buyer's employees
directly involved in evaluating the Transaction, in each case
who have a need to know such information. Buyer shall cause each
Representative to comply with this Agreement and shall be liable
for any breach by any Representative as if such breach were
committed by Buyer itself.
条款 05Clause 05

期限与存续 Term and Survival

Term and Survival Risk Level: MED
CLAUSE 05 中风险MED RISK

买方倾向于将NDA保密义务限制在2年甚至更短，而卖方披露的核心商业秘密（如客户名单、定价模型、技术路线图）往往具有更长的经济价值周期。如果协议到期，买方可以在2年后自由使用这些信息，即使当时交易早已失败。

谈判策略：将保密义务期限设定为5年；对于明确标注为"贸易秘密"（Trade Secret）的信息，要求保密义务永久存续，直至该信息进入公知领域为止；同时约定协议终止不影响此前已产生的任何权利和义务。

Buyers prefer to cap confidentiality obligations at two years or less — but the core trade secrets sellers disclose (customer lists, pricing models, technology roadmaps) often retain economic value far longer. Once the term expires, buyers may freely use the information even if the deal collapsed years prior.

Negotiation strategy: Set the confidentiality period at five years. For information expressly designated "Trade Secret," require obligations to survive indefinitely until that information enters the public domain. Include a savings clause: termination of this Agreement shall not affect any right or obligation accrued prior to termination.

// Seller-Friendly Redline
The obligations under this Agreement shall remain in effect for
five (5) years from the date hereof; provided that, with respect
to any information designated as a "Trade Secret" by Seller in
writing, such obligations shall survive indefinitely until such
Trade Secret enters the public domain through no act or omission
of Buyer or its Representatives.
条款 06Clause 06

违约救济 Remedies for Breach

Remedies for Breach Risk Level: MED
CLAUSE 06 中风险MED RISK

买方草案往往将救济限制为金钱赔偿，排除禁令救济（injunctive relief）。而机密信息一旦泄露，金钱赔偿往往难以弥补实际损失——竞争对手已获知您的客户名单或技术秘密，事后的赔偿无法消除这一损害。禁令救济才是卖方最有效的保护手段。

谈判策略：明确约定双方承认金钱赔偿不足以弥补违约损失，卖方有权申请禁令或其他衡平法救济，且无需提供担保；同时要求买方预先承认违约将造成难以量化的不可弥补损害。

Buyer drafts commonly limit remedies to monetary damages and exclude injunctive relief. But once confidential information is disclosed, money rarely makes the seller whole — a competitor has already learned your customer list or technical secrets. Injunctive relief is the seller's most effective protection.

Negotiation strategy: Expressly provide that monetary damages are inadequate and that seller is entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief without posting bond. Require buyer to pre-acknowledge that breach will cause irreparable harm not susceptible to monetary quantification.

// Seller-Friendly Redline
Buyer acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement would cause
irreparable harm to Seller for which monetary damages would be an
inadequate remedy. Accordingly, Seller shall be entitled to seek
injunctive relief, specific performance, and other equitable remedies
in addition to all remedies available at law or in equity, without
the requirement to post any bond or other security.
条款 07Clause 07

管辖法律 Governing Law

Governing Law Risk Level: LOW
CLAUSE 07 低风险LOW RISK

管辖法律条款通常被视为常规条款，但选择错误的司法管辖区可能显著增加卖方的诉讼成本和执行难度。如果买方位于纽约而卖方在加州，买方草案可能要求适用纽约法律并在纽约诉讼，这对卖方而言意味着更高的律师费和出行成本。

谈判策略：优先争取以卖方所在州法律为准据法；若买方坚持特定司法管辖，应评估该地区对商业秘密和禁令救济的保护力度——特拉华州和纽约州通常对这类条款有成熟的判例法支撑。

Governing law is often treated as boilerplate, but choosing the wrong jurisdiction can significantly raise litigation costs and enforcement barriers. If buyer is in New York and seller in California, a buyer draft requiring New York law and litigation means higher attorney fees and travel costs for seller.

Negotiation strategy: Prioritize the seller's home state as governing law. If buyer insists on a specific forum, evaluate that jurisdiction's trade secret protections and injunctive relief standards — Delaware and New York both have well-developed case law supporting these provisions.

// Seller-Friendly Redline
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of
[Seller's State], without regard to conflict of laws principles.
Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of
the state and federal courts located in [Seller's County, State]
for resolution of any dispute arising out of or relating to
this Agreement.
条款 08Clause 08

知识产权保护 IP Protection

IP Protection Risk Level: MED
CLAUSE 08 中风险MED RISK

许多NDA仅规定保密义务，但未明确披露机密信息不授予任何知识产权许可。若没有这一条款，买方在尽职调查中接触卖方的专有技术后，可能主张隐含许可（implied license）——即认为卖方的披露行为默许了买方使用相关技术。

谈判策略：明确约定NDA中的任何披露均不构成、也不应被解释为对任何专利、商标、著作权或其他知识产权的授权；买方接收信息的唯一目的是评估交易可行性，不得将其用于任何其他用途。

Many NDAs address confidentiality but fail to disclaim IP licenses. Without an express disclaimer, a buyer who receives seller's proprietary technology during diligence may argue an implied license — claiming that seller's disclosure implicitly authorized use of the technology.

Negotiation strategy: Expressly provide that no disclosure under this Agreement constitutes or shall be construed as a grant of any license under any patent, trademark, copyright, or other intellectual property right. Buyer's receipt of information is solely for evaluating the Transaction.

// Seller-Friendly Redline
Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting Buyer
any license, right, title, or interest in or to any patent,
trademark, copyright, trade secret, or other intellectual property
of Seller. All Confidential Information is disclosed solely for
the purpose of evaluating the potential Transaction and for no
other purpose whatsoever.
签署前Pre-Signing

检查清单 Checklist

  • 机密信息定义以书面交付为原则，口头披露须48小时内书面确认
  • 独立开发例外附有同期文件证明义务
  • 停滞条款已删除"don't-ask-don't-waive"语言
  • 停滞期限明确（如12个月），收到第三方要约后自动解除
  • 竞业禁止/非招揽已从NDA中剥离，留待SPA谈判
  • 代表范围已明确列举，买方对代表违约承担连带责任
  • 保密义务期限不少于5年，贸易秘密永久存续
  • 已明确约定禁令救济权，买方预先承认违约构成不可弥补损害
  • 管辖法律为卖方所在州，诉讼地点对卖方有利
  • 已明确排除隐含知识产权许可，用途限于评估交易
  • Confidential Information defaults to written delivery; oral disclosures confirmed in writing within 48 hours
  • Independent development exception requires contemporaneous written documentation
  • Standstill clause: "don't-ask-don't-waive" language removed
  • Standstill period is fixed (e.g., 12 months) and terminates on receipt of third-party proposal
  • Non-compete and non-solicit removed from NDA; reserved for definitive SPA
  • Representatives enumerated by category; Buyer liable for Representatives' breaches
  • Confidentiality term at least 5 years; trade secrets survive indefinitely
  • Injunctive relief expressly preserved; Buyer pre-acknowledges irreparable harm
  • Governing law is Seller's home state; forum favorable to Seller
  • No implied IP license; use of information limited to Transaction evaluation
