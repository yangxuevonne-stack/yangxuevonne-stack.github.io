一市场背景：一个全新的债权交易市场

2026年2月20日，美国最高法院在 Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump 案中以6:3裁定IEEPA关税违宪。这一裁决在法律上确立了一件事：自2025年2月4日（芬太尼关税）和2025年4月2日（对等关税）起，至2026年2月24日止，超过33万家进口商支付的累计约1660亿至1750亿美元关税，理论上应予退还。

然而，最高法院的裁决并未说明退款的时间、流程和金额确认方式。美国国际贸易法院（CIT）随后于2026年3月4日发布了要求CBP退款的"普遍退款令"，但CBP在3月6日以系统无法处理如此体量（5300万条目）为由申请暂缓执行，并开始开发名为CAPE（Consolidated Administration and Processing of Entries）的专用退款系统。

现状（2026年4月）：CAPE系统目前约73%完成，退款流程尚未启动。CBP估计CAPE将完成实际退款处理，但时间表不确定，业界普遍预计可能需要数月至数年。比较参照：类似的Harbor Maintenance Tax（HMT）退款在法院命令后花费了4年才完成处理，而IEEPA退款规模是HMT的200倍以上。

正是在这一背景下，一个以往主要存在于破产债权和诉讼融资领域的交易模式，开始在IEEPA退税领域迅速扩张：进口商将其退税申请权利（参与权）以折扣价出售给银行、对冲基金或专业债权收购机构，换取当下的确定性流动性。

这个市场的参与者包括：Texas Capital Bank（公开声明已介入IEEPA退税权利市场）、各类专注于不良资产和债权交易的对冲基金、以及AES（Asset Enhancement Solutions）等专业债务顾问机构。在最高法院裁决后不到六周，AES已促成超过2000万美元的IEEPA退税权利变现交易。

"这不是慈善。买家进入这个市场是因为他们相信能在折扣买入价与最终退款金额之间赚取可观回报。出售方理解这个逻辑，是谈判出好条款的第一步。"

IMarket Background: A New Claims Trading Market

On February 20, 2026, the U.S. Supreme Court ruled 6-3 in Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump that IEEPA does not authorize the President to impose tariffs. This established that approximately $166–175 billion in tariffs paid by over 330,000 importers between early February 2025 and February 24, 2026 are theoretically subject to refund.

However, the Court provided no roadmap for how or when refunds would be distributed. The Court of International Trade issued a Universal Refund Order on March 4, 2026, but CBP obtained a stay on March 6, citing the impossibility of processing 53 million entries through existing systems. CBP began developing the CAPE (Consolidated Administration and Processing of Entries) system specifically for this purpose.

Current Status (April 2026): The CAPE system is approximately 73% complete. Actual refund payments have not yet commenced. Timeline remains uncertain — industry observers estimate months to years before importers receive funds. Comparable reference point: the Harbor Maintenance Tax universal refund order took four years to implement. The IEEPA refund is 200+ times the scale of HMT.

Against this backdrop, a transaction model familiar in bankruptcy claims and litigation finance has moved rapidly into the IEEPA tariff space: importers sell their refund claim participation rights at a discount to banks, hedge funds, and specialized claim purchasers in exchange for immediate, certain liquidity.

Market participants include Texas Capital Bank (which has publicly announced its involvement in purchasing IEEPA refund claims), distressed-debt hedge funds, and advisory firms such as Asset Enhancement Solutions (AES), which facilitated over $20 million in IEEPA claim monetization transactions within six weeks of the Supreme Court ruling.

"This is not charity. Buyers enter this market because they believe they can earn an attractive return on the spread between their discounted purchase price and the eventual refund. Sellers who understand this logic are in a far stronger negotiating position."

二交易结构：参与权 vs 完全转让

在IEEPA退税权利的变现交易中，最核心的结构性问题是：出售什么？实践中有两种主要模式，选择哪种对出售方的权利保护、配合义务和风险敞口影响巨大。

模式A（主流）

经济参与权出售（Participation）

进口商保留申请人身份，即仍以自己名义向CBP/CIT申请退税，但将最终收到的退款金额（全部或部分）的经济权益，转让给买方。买方支付折扣价购买这一经济利益。

这是市场上的主流结构，原因是：关税退款权利在法律上通常不可自由转让给第三方（只有进口商记录人才有向CBP申请退款的资格），因此必须通过参与权的形式实现经济利益的转移，而非权利本身的转让。

模式B（较少见）

完全转让（Full Assignment）

理论上，进口商可以尝试将其对政府的退税请求权完整转让。但这面临重大法律障碍：《美国法典》31 U.S.C. § 3727（Assignment of Claims Act）对政府债权的转让设有严格限制，转让通常需要经过特定的行政程序才能对政府生效。

在IEEPA退税场景中，完全转让在法律上存在重大疑问。即便各方签署了转让协议，CBP也只会向记录在案的进口商退款，而非受让方。

⚠️ 结构选择的核心含义：因为主流结构是参与权而非完全转让，进口商在出售后仍须承担申请退税的积极义务——包括向ACE/CAPE系统提交申报、配合文件准备、响应CBP的质疑等。这意味着"卖掉就不管了"的预期是错误的。合同必须精确界定出售方在申请过程中的配合义务范围和成本承担。

2.1混合结构：部分出售保留Upside

对于退税金额较大的进口商，还有一种值得考虑的结构：出售部分经济参与权，保留另一部分作为自留份额（skin in the game）。例如，出售70%的经济权益获得即时流动性，保留30%等待实际退款。

这一结构对出售方有两个好处：第一，如果退款最终超出预期（包括利息收入），出售方可以分享Upside；第二，出售方保留部分权益也向买方传达了一个信号——出售方对权利的有效性有信心，这有助于提高买方的出价。

IITransaction Structure: Participation vs. Full Assignment

The central structural question in any IEEPA refund claim monetization is: what exactly is being sold? The choice between a participation structure and a full assignment has significant implications for the seller's ongoing obligations, risk exposure, and future upside.

Model A (Market Standard)

Economic Participation Sale

The importer retains its status as named claimant and continues to file with CBP/CAPE/CIT in its own name. It sells the economic interest in the eventual refund proceeds (all or a portion) to the buyer at a discount. The buyer pays the purchase price for this economic participation.

This is the dominant market structure because tariff refund rights are generally not freely transferable to third parties under U.S. customs law — only the importer of record has legal standing to claim a refund from CBP. Economic interest in proceeds can be transferred; the underlying legal right typically cannot.

Model B (Rare)

Full Assignment

A complete transfer of the refund claim to a third party. This faces a significant legal obstacle: the Assignment of Claims Act (31 U.S.C. § 3727) imposes strict requirements on assignments of government claims. Even if the parties execute a full assignment agreement, CBP will only issue refunds to the importer of record — not to any purported assignee.

In the IEEPA context, full assignment raises substantial legal questions. Sellers should not agree to a "full assignment" structure without careful legal analysis of whether such an assignment is effective against CBP.

⚠️ Critical Structural Implication: Because the market structure is participation rather than assignment, the importer retains active obligations to pursue the refund claim after the sale — including submitting filings through ACE/CAPE, preparing documentation, and responding to CBP inquiries. The expectation that "selling means walking away" is incorrect. The agreement must precisely define the seller's cooperation obligations and who bears the associated costs.

2.1Hybrid Structure: Partial Sale with Retained Upside

For importers with substantial refund amounts, a hybrid structure deserves consideration: sell a portion of the economic participation while retaining a self-retained share. For example, sell 70% of the economic interest for immediate liquidity while retaining 30% to participate in the eventual refund.

This structure offers two advantages to sellers: first, if the refund ultimately exceeds projections (including statutory interest), the seller participates in the upside; second, the seller's retained stake signals confidence in claim validity — which typically allows the seller to negotiate a higher price on the portion sold.

四出售前的准备：如何最大化定价

专业买家会对你的权利进行深度尽调——他们的目的是找到压价的理由。你作为出售方应当先于买方完成内部的"自我尽调"，消除压价依据，并主动展示权利的质量。

准备阶段 出售前必须完成的六项准备工作
  • 完成CBP ACH退款系统注册。这是领取退税的前提条件。未注册的进口商，即便最终胜诉，退款也会被CBP拒绝发放。这一步直接影响权利的可变现性，是买方尽调的第一个检查项。
  • 汇总所有IEEPA关税支付记录。从CBP的ACE系统下载全部相关进口条目，确认每笔条目的IEEPA关税金额、支付日期、关税分类编码（HTS code）。这个数据集是权利估值的基础。
  • 确认每笔条目的清算状态。将条目分类为：(1) 未清算；(2) 已清算但仍在180天抗议期内；(3) 已最终清算超过180天。第三类条目的退税权利存在最大不确定性，需要单独分析。
  • 评估下游转嫁情况。检查2025年4月至2026年2月期间对买家的价格调整记录。如有明确的书面记录将关税成本作为提价依据，该部分退税权利面临下游索赔风险——在定价中应有所反映，并在销售文件中明确处理。
  • 保留权利的法律程序。对已清算条目提交行政抗议（protest）；考虑向CIT提起诉讼以保留所有渠道的权利。未采取保全措施的权利在买家眼中价值显著打折。
  • 梳理下游索赔敞口。是否已收到客户要求分享退税的函件或诉讼？这些敞口将是买方谈判的重要筹码，应提前完成法律评估并确定合理处理方案。

IVPre-Sale Preparation: Maximizing Your Price

Sophisticated buyers will conduct thorough due diligence specifically designed to identify reasons to reduce their offer price. Sellers should complete an internal "self-due-diligence" before approaching any buyer — eliminating pressure points and proactively demonstrating claim quality.

Preparation Phase Six Pre-Sale Actions That Maximize Pricing
  • Complete CBP ACH refund enrollment. This is a prerequisite for receiving any refund. Importers who have not enrolled will have refunds rejected by CBP even if they prevail in litigation. This is the first item on any buyer's due diligence checklist.
  • Compile all IEEPA duty payment records. Download all relevant import entries from CBP's ACE system, confirming the IEEPA duty amount, payment date, and HTS code for each entry. This dataset is the foundation of claim valuation.
  • Confirm liquidation status of each entry. Categorize entries as: (1) unliquidated; (2) liquidated but within the 180-day protest window; (3) finally liquidated beyond 180 days. The third category carries the greatest recovery uncertainty and requires separate analysis.
  • Assess downstream pass-through exposure. Review pricing communications to customers between April 2025 and February 2026. Written documentation explicitly attributing price increases to IEEPA tariffs creates downstream claim risk — this must be reflected in pricing and addressed explicitly in the sale documentation.
  • Preserve legal rights. File administrative protests for liquidated entries within the 180-day window; consider filing suit at CIT to preserve all recovery channels. Unpresented rights are materially discounted by buyers.
  • Map downstream claim exposure. Have you received letters or lawsuits from customers asserting entitlement to share in tariff refunds? This exposure is a key negotiating leverage point for buyers and must be assessed before entering negotiations.

VKey Negotiation Terms — Point by Point

The following covers the most seller-consequential provisions in an IEEPA refund claim Participation Agreement, with market practice reference points and negotiation guidance.

1
购买价格、定价基础与Upside分享机制Purchase Price, Pricing Basis & Upside Sharing

这是谈判的核心。购买价格通常表述为"确认权利金额（Confirmed Claim Amount）的X%"。卖方的目标是：(1) 最大化X的百分比；(2) 确保"确认权利金额"的定义对卖方有利；(3) 争取超出预期退款部分（Upside）的分享权。

卖方理想立场
购买价格基于退税申请金额的70%以上；若实际退款（含利息）超出估算，超出部分按约定比例（如30%）给卖方。利息收入全额归卖方。
买方标准立场
购买价格基于保守估算的"净回收金额"（扣除追索成本、诉讼费用、潜在下游索赔后）；无Upside分享；利息归买方。
市场惯例参考
当前市场主流：面值的50%~65%，高质量权利可达70%+；部分交易含Upside分享条款（触发条件：实际退款超出估算基础的115%~120%）。

谈判要点：买方通常会在购买价格计算基础（"确认权利金额"的定义）上做文章，将各类不确定因素折算进去。卖方应坚持：确认权利金额 = 进口商实际支付的IEEPA关税总额，不应被买方单方面打折。若买方要求对某些条目（如已最终清算条目）作出折扣，应要求该折扣有明确的量化依据，而非笼统的百分比折扣。

This is the core of the negotiation. The purchase price is typically expressed as "X% of the Confirmed Claim Amount." The seller's objectives are: (1) maximize X; (2) ensure the definition of "Confirmed Claim Amount" is seller-favorable; (3) negotiate for upside sharing if actual refunds exceed projections.

Seller's Ideal Position
Purchase price ≥70% of claim amount; if actual refund (including interest) exceeds estimates, excess shared with seller at an agreed ratio (e.g., 30%). Interest income allocated entirely to seller.
Buyer's Standard Position
Purchase price based on conservative "net recoverable amount" (after deducting pursuit costs, litigation expenses, contingency reserves for downstream claims). No upside sharing. Interest to buyer.
Market Practice Reference
Current market: 50%–65% of face value, with high-quality claims reaching 70%+. Some transactions include upside sharing triggers (typically: actual refund exceeds 115%–120% of the estimated base).

Negotiation Leverage: Buyers often compress the "Confirmed Claim Amount" by embedding discounts for uncertainty into the base calculation. Sellers should insist that Confirmed Claim Amount = total IEEPA duties actually paid by the importer of record, without unilateral buyer discounts. Where a buyer demands haircuts on specific entry categories (e.g., finally liquidated entries), require explicit, quantified justification rather than a blended percentage reduction.

2
非追索性与陈述保证的范围限定Non-Recourse Structure & Limiting Representations

出售方最重要的保护机制是确保交易结构为非追索性（Non-Recourse）——即买方购买的是权利，而不是向卖方提供有保障的借款。如果退款因政府政策、法院程序延误或其他外部原因迟迟无法到账，买方不能向卖方追索已支付的购买价格。

卖方理想立场
协议明确约定：非追索性（Non-Recourse）。唯一例外：若卖方存在欺诈、故意虚假陈述或故意隐瞒已知会导致权利无效的重大事实，买方方可追索。
买方常见要求
要求更宽泛的追索条件：包括任何陈述或保证的违反（即便是善意错误）；要求对不可追索有较高的确认门槛和知情程度要求。
市场惯例参考
这类交易通常约定为非追索性，除卖方欺诈或重大故意虚假陈述外。善意错误不构成追索依据——这是出售方保护的核心底线。

陈述保证范围的谈判：买方通常要求出售方对以下事项作出保证：(1) 卖方是相关进口条目的记录在案的进口商；(2) 关税已实际支付；(3) 卖方知悉的会导致权利无效的重大事项已充分披露；(4) 没有已知的在先转让（没有把同一权利卖给其他人）。

卖方应避免：(1) 保证退款的最终金额；(2) 保证退款的时间表；(3) 保证下游不存在任何索赔（这超出了卖方的控制范围）。这些不确定性正是买方获得折扣的对价，不应反过来成为卖方的义务。

The most important seller protection is ensuring the transaction is structured as genuinely non-recourse — the buyer is purchasing a right, not making a secured loan. If the refund is delayed or reduced due to government policy, court proceedings, or other external factors, the buyer cannot pursue the seller for return of the purchase price.

Seller's Ideal Position
Agreement expressly states: Non-Recourse. The sole exception: fraud, intentional misrepresentation, or deliberate concealment of known facts that invalidate the claim. Good-faith errors are not a basis for recourse.
Buyer's Typical Ask
Broader recourse triggers: any breach of representation or warranty (including good-faith errors). Higher confirmation threshold before non-recourse protection activates.
Market Practice Reference
These transactions are generally non-recourse except for seller fraud or intentional misrepresentation. Good-faith errors should not give rise to recourse — this is the seller's essential floor.

Scoping Representations: Buyers typically require representations covering: (1) seller is the importer of record for the relevant entries; (2) IEEPA duties were actually paid; (3) all known material facts that could invalidate the claim have been disclosed; (4) no prior assignment of the same claim to another party. Sellers should resist: (1) guaranteeing the ultimate refund amount; (2) guaranteeing timing; (3) representing that no downstream claims exist. These uncertainties are precisely what the buyer's discount compensates for — they should not become seller obligations.

3
申请控制权与配合义务的边界Claim Control & Cooperation Obligation Boundaries

由于进口商在出售后仍须以自己名义提起退税申请，买卖双方必须明确界定：谁有权控制申请策略、谁承担申请成本、以及配合义务的具体范围。

卖方理想立场
卖方保留对申请策略的最终决定权；买方仅可提出建议；申请过程中的所有合理费用（律师费、顾问费、行政费用）由买方承担；配合义务明确有上限（如每月不超过X小时的管理层时间）。
买方常见要求
买方对申请策略有主导权（因为买方有更强的经济激励和专业能力）；卖方承担自身的行政配合成本；配合义务无明确上限。
市场惯例参考
通常由买方主导申请策略，但需经卖方同意不得取消权利或接受显著不利的和解（如退款金额低于已付购买价格）；申请成本由买方承担；卖方配合义务有合理边界。

核心谈判点：和解权限是最重要的保护。卖方应确保：不论买方主导申请，买方不得在未经卖方同意的情况下接受任何导致卖方自留份额受损的和解。即便卖方已将全部经济权益出售，和解决定可能影响卖方的法律地位和下游索赔风险。

Because the importer remains the named claimant after the sale, both parties must clearly define who controls the claim strategy, who bears costs, and what the seller's cooperation obligation entails.

Seller's Ideal Position
Seller retains ultimate decision-making authority over claim strategy; buyer may advise but not direct; all reasonable pursuit costs (legal fees, advisory fees, filing costs) borne by buyer; seller cooperation capped (e.g., no more than X management hours per month).
Buyer's Typical Ask
Buyer controls claim strategy (buyer has stronger economic incentive and specialized expertise); seller bears its own administrative cooperation costs; no defined limit on cooperation obligations.
Market Practice Reference
Buyer typically directs claim strategy, but seller consent is required before any settlement that materially reduces claim value or any abandonment of the claim; pursuit costs borne by buyer; seller cooperation obligation is bounded and reasonable.

Critical Point — Settlement Authority: The seller must ensure that regardless of who controls the pursuit strategy, the buyer cannot accept any settlement that (a) reduces the seller's retained economic interest without seller consent, or (b) creates adverse legal consequences for the seller in downstream litigation. This protection matters even if the seller has sold 100% of the economic participation.

4
下游索赔风险分配：关键中的关键Downstream Claims Risk Allocation: The Critical Issue

这是2026年IEEPA退税变现市场中最复杂、也最被出售方低估的风险点。如前所述，大量进口商曾将IEEPA关税成本通过提价转嫁给下游买家。在最高法院裁决后，下游买家开始以"不当得利"为由对进口商提起诉讼，主张进口商取得的政府退款应当部分归还给下游。

当进口商将退税参与权出售给机构买家后，这个法律风险并未消失——退税收到了，但诉讼索赔的对象仍是进口商。合同中必须明确这一风险由谁承担。

卖方理想立场
买方知晓并接受下游索赔风险是其收购折扣所对价的风险之一；若因下游索赔导致退款实际金额减少，购买价格无须调整（非追索性原则的延伸）；买方协助卖方应对下游索赔的诉讼防御费用由买方承担。
买方常见要求
出售方对下游索赔作出全面披露；若实际退款因下游索赔减少，购买价格相应调整（即卖方承担下游风险）；卖方对所有下游索赔提供赔偿保证（indemnity）。
市场惯例参考
下游索赔风险目前仍在市场形成中，尚无完全统一的惯例。倾向于：已知的和已具体化的下游索赔需充分披露；未知/非具体的下游索赔风险折入买方定价；不要求卖方对一般性下游风险提供无上限赔偿。

实务建议：卖方应坚持在协议中明确区分：(1) 已披露的具体下游索赔（买方已知，折入定价）；(2) 签署后新产生的下游索赔（卖方不承担责任）；(3) 因卖方故意隐瞒而被买方认定无效的权利（这是唯一应当由卖方承担的风险）。

This is the most complex and most underestimated risk point in 2026 IEEPA refund monetization transactions. As discussed, a wave of downstream class action lawsuits has already been filed against importers who passed tariff costs to customers and are now pursuing refunds. When the importer sells its refund participation to a buyer, this legal risk does not transfer — the government pays the refund, but the downstream lawsuit targets the importer.

Seller's Ideal Position
Buyer acknowledges and accepts downstream claim risk as a component of the discount at which it purchased; if downstream litigation reduces the net refund, no purchase price adjustment (non-recourse principle applies); buyer contributes to defense costs for downstream claims arising from the purchased claim pool.
Buyer's Typical Ask
Full seller disclosure of all downstream claims; purchase price adjustment if downstream litigation reduces actual refund; seller indemnification of buyer for downstream claims.
Market Practice Reference
This risk allocation is still being negotiated in the market. Prevailing direction: known, specific downstream claims must be disclosed; unknown/non-specific downstream risk is priced into the buyer's discount rate; sellers should not provide open-ended indemnification for generalized downstream risk.

Practical Advice: Sellers should insist on a three-tier framework in the agreement: (1) disclosed specific downstream claims (buyer has knowledge, reflected in pricing); (2) downstream claims arising after signing (seller bears no responsibility); (3) claims invalidated due to deliberate seller concealment (the only category where seller should accept liability).

5
利息收入的归属Allocation of Statutory Interest

这是一个经常被忽视但金额可观的条款。根据美国联邦法律，政府对退款金额通常须支付利息（联邦法定利率，参见19 U.S.C. § 1505(c)及相关规定）。IEEPA关税从2025年4月缴纳至2026年，利息积累金额相当可观。

卖方理想立场
利息全额归卖方。理由：买方已按折扣价购得本金退税权益的经济参与，利息是卖方当初被迫垫付关税的资金成本补偿，不应归买方。
买方常见立场
利息随参与权的购买一并归买方（即买方认为利息是其购买"全部退款权利"的一部分）。
市场惯例参考
有争议。合理的出发点：将利息视为卖方保留项，并与购买价格分开谈判。若买方坚持要求获得利息，则卖方应要求购买价格相应上调（提高百分点）作为交换。

This is a frequently overlooked but financially significant provision. Under U.S. federal law, the government typically owes interest on duty refunds (federal statutory rate; see 19 U.S.C. § 1505(c)). IEEPA duties paid from April 2025 through February 2026 have accrued meaningful interest that adds to the total refund value.

Seller's Ideal Position
Statutory interest allocated entirely to seller. Rationale: the buyer purchased a discounted economic participation in the principal refund; the interest compensates the importer for the cost of capital tied up in duty payments — it should not transfer with the discount purchase.
Buyer's Position
Interest follows the participation — i.e., buyer treats interest as part of the "full refund rights" it purchased at a discount.
Market Practice Reference
Contested. Sellers should treat interest as a seller-retained item and negotiate it separately from the purchase price. If the buyer insists on interest, the seller should require a corresponding upward adjustment to the purchase percentage in exchange.
6
失败场景保护：权利无法追索时的安排Failure Scenario Protection: When the Claim Cannot Be Recovered

IEEPA退税仍存在不确定性——尽管CIT已下令退款，但政府仍可能上诉，或CAPE系统长期无法运行。出售方必须确保在最坏情况下，风险已被买方知情地承担。

  • 明确购买价格的不可退还性：协议应明确约定，无论退税是否最终到账、何时到账，买方已支付的购买价格不得要求退还——这是非追索性结构的核心保障。
  • 超时终止权：如果退款在约定的最长等待期（如24个月）内仍未到账，允许双方终止协议（各自保留已取得权利，不互相追索）。这保护卖方不被无限期的配合义务套牢。
  • 政策逆转保护：若国会立法或行政令重新取消退款权利（虽然可能性极低，但不可忽视），明确约定该风险由买方承担。

IEEPA refund recovery remains uncertain. Despite the CIT Universal Refund Order, the government could appeal, the CAPE system could remain non-operational for extended periods, or Congress could legislate on the issue. Sellers must ensure that worst-case scenario risk has been knowingly assumed by the buyer.

  • Non-refundability of purchase price: The agreement should expressly state that the purchase price is non-refundable regardless of whether and when the refund is ultimately received. This is the core of the non-recourse protection.
  • Long-stop termination right: If the refund has not been received within a defined maximum period (e.g., 24 months), both parties may terminate the agreement without mutual recourse. This protects the seller from being indefinitely bound by cooperation obligations.
  • Policy reversal protection: If Congressional action or executive order retroactively eliminates refund rights (unlikely but not impossible), this risk should expressly fall on the buyer.

六隐藏的法律风险：出售方必须了解

6.1"双重补偿"的公众关系与诉讼风险

Ballard Spahr的分析揭示了一个正在快速发展的诉讼风险：如果进口商(1)将IEEPA关税成本转嫁给了消费者（通过提价），并且(2)同时向政府申请退税，原告律师将主张进口商"双重补偿"——既从消费者处收回了成本，又将从政府处收到退款。这类集体诉讼正在多个行业快速蔓延。

对于出售退税参与权的进口商，这个风险尤为值得关注，因为：一旦退税款项到账（即便立刻被转付给了买方），进口商仍会成为下游诉讼的目标。在出售参与权的合同谈判中，下游风险的分配必须是一个明确谈判的条款（见条款4），而非留白。

6.2出售参与权可能触发"双重出售"风险

若进口商曾向多家潜在买家披露过权利信息并接受过Term Sheet（即便未最终签约），在最终成交后，落选的买方可能主张某种权利。协议中应包含卖方对在先承诺和承诺的陈述，且签约前应确认不存在与其他方的竞争性未了承诺。

6.3出售行为本身可能成为下游诉讼的证据

若进口商已将关税成本转嫁给下游，而随后以较高折扣率出售退税参与权（隐含了对退税成功可能性的较高确认），这一行为可能被下游原告解读为"进口商在主张大额退税前已知道退税会实现，但未告知其客户"——从而强化下游诉讼的论点。在出售参与权之前，应与律师评估这一信息披露风险。

🚫 特别提示：不要在公开声明（earnings call、投资者披露、网站FAQ）中宣布正在积极追索IEEPA退税，同时又声称价格上涨是由关税驱动的。原告律师正在系统性地检索这类矛盾陈述并在诉状中援引。如果你正在考虑出售参与权，请先与法律顾问对齐公开披露策略。

VIHidden Legal Risks: What Sellers Must Know

6.1The "Double Recovery" Class Action Wave

A rapidly developing litigation risk: importers who (1) passed IEEPA tariff costs through to consumers via price increases, and (2) are now seeking government refunds, face "double recovery" class action claims — plaintiffs allege that having been made whole by customers, the importer should not also receive a government refund. At least five law firms had filed such suits within four weeks of the Supreme Court ruling.

For sellers of refund participations, this risk is particularly salient: even if the refund proceeds are immediately forwarded to the buyer upon receipt, the importer remains the litigation target. Downstream risk allocation in the Participation Agreement (Term 4 above) cannot be left unaddressed.

6.2Risk of "Prior Commitment" Claims

If the seller approached multiple potential buyers and provided Term Sheets or LOIs before finalizing with one buyer, unsuccessful bidders may assert some form of claim. The Participation Agreement should include representations that no prior competing commitments exist, and the seller should conduct a clean-up of any outstanding term sheets before signing.

6.3The Sale Transaction Itself as Evidence in Downstream Litigation

If an importer passed tariff costs downstream and then sold its refund participation at a meaningful rate (implying high confidence in recovery), downstream plaintiffs may argue this transaction demonstrates that the importer knew a substantial refund was coming while its customers were paying inflated prices. Before executing a participation sale, discuss information disclosure strategy with counsel.

🚫 Specific Warning: Do not make public statements (earnings calls, investor disclosures, website FAQs) claiming you are actively pursuing large IEEPA refunds while simultaneously having attributed price increases to tariff costs. Plaintiffs' counsel are systematically monitoring these contradictory statements. If you are considering a participation sale, align your public disclosure strategy with legal counsel first.

七会计与税务处理

这类交易的财务报告处理尚未形成完全统一的行业共识，但以下是目前观察到的市场倾向：

会计处理 参与权出售的财务报告处理

通常按"借款"而非"收益"处理。由于退税本身的收取仍不确定，出售参与权取得的款项在很多情况下被视为有条件的预付，而非已实现的收益。这意味着在资产负债表上可能体现为负债，而非即时利润。

IEEPA关税的历史处理：在并购尽调（Quality of Earnings分析）中，历史IEEPA关税支出通常被列为非经常性支出，但对应的退税权利不被纳入历史EBITDA调整，而是作为"或有利好"在交易结构中单独处理。

税务层面：退款收到时通常构成应税收入。若出售参与权收到的款项被定性为借款，则收款时可能不立即产生税务义务；若被定性为收益，则可能立即触发应税事件。这一区分对现金流规划影响显著，应提前与税务顾问确认。

利息收入：政府支付的利息通常按普通收入征税。

VIIAccounting & Tax Treatment

Accounting Treatment Financial Reporting for Participation Sales

Typically treated as a borrowing, not a gain. Because the underlying refund remains contingent, proceeds from a participation sale are in many cases characterized as a conditional advance rather than realized income. This means the proceeds may appear as a liability on the balance sheet rather than immediate profit — with revenue recognition deferred until the refund is actually received.

Historical IEEPA tariff costs: In M&A due diligence (Quality of Earnings analysis), historical IEEPA tariff expenditures are typically treated as non-recurring costs, while the corresponding refund rights are not included in historical EBITDA adjustments — instead treated as contingent upside addressed through transaction structuring (price adjustments, escrows, specific refund-sharing provisions).

Tax implications: Refunds received are generally taxable income. If the participation sale proceeds are characterized as a borrowing, they may not trigger an immediate tax liability; if characterized as a gain, they may be taxable upon receipt of proceeds. This distinction materially affects cash flow planning and should be confirmed with tax counsel before signing.

Statutory interest: Government-paid interest on duty refunds is generally taxed as ordinary income.
