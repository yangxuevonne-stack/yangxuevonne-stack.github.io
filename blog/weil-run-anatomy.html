怎么读这三条曲线How to read the curves

Kirkland 对业务量陡峭，对年资平坦。创收翻倍，薪酬翻倍。资深但业务一般的合伙人可能根本不在权益层里。

Kirkland is steep on originations, flat on tenure. Double the book, double the pay. A senior partner with moderate originations may not be in the equity tier at all.

Cravath 对年资陡峭，对业务量几乎平坦。资深稳健型在这里拿得最多。创收王则要比在 Kirkland 少拿四到五成。

Cravath is steep on tenure, nearly flat on originations. The senior steady earns the most here. The rainmaker earns 40–50% less than at Kirkland.

Weil 夹在两者之间，而池子更小。结果是创收王和资深稳健型相对各自的「天然归宿」都被低估了。

Weil sits in between, with a smaller pool. The result: both the rainmaker and the senior steady are underpriced relative to their natural home.

为什么早走才是理性的 Why leaving early is the rational move

一个简化的 Weil：250 名合伙人，20 亿美元营收，6 亿美元的律师、员工与租约成本。创收王每人带来约 5,000 万。其余所有人从剩下的里面分。

A simplified Weil: 250 partners, $2B revenue, $600M in lawyer salaries, staff and leases. Each rainmaker brings about $50M. Everyone else splits what is left.

为什么会加速Why it accelerates

固定成本不会跟着走。租约、律师工资、行政人员都留着，营收却走了 — Weil 刚签下新总部。每一次离职造成的伤害都比上一次大。

Fixed costs don't leave. Leases, associate salaries, support staff — they stay while revenue walks out. Weil had just signed a new headquarters. Each departure is more damaging than the last.

合伙人是所有者。利润跌 10%，当年收入就少 10%，没有缓冲期。最后走的人背锅。残余债务和租约担保，落在那时还在、还能签字的人头上。

Partners are owners. A 10% profit drop means 10% less income, with no buffer period. The last ones out hold the bag. Residual obligations and lease guarantees land on whoever is still there to sign.

而且没有任何东西能刹车And nothing can stop it

执业行为规则第 5.6 条禁止限制律师离职后执业。2025 年 8 月纽约市律协更进一步：可没收的奖金、递延薪酬、资本金扣减，只要起到阻碍竞业的作用，同样不被允许。一旦算术翻转，纽约的律所没有刹车可踩。

Model Rule 5.6 bars any restriction on a lawyer's right to practise after leaving. The NYC Bar went further in August 2025: forfeitable bonuses, deferred comp, capital deductions — if they deter competition, they are impermissible too. Once the arithmetic flips, there is no brake to pull.

第四幕 · 没有护城河Act IV · No Moat

律师留不住 A lawyer cannot be held

一家没有金库的银行。存款是合伙人的客户关系，每个储户都能在任何一天全额取走，而且先走的那个不受任何惩罚。

A bank with no vault. The deposits are the partners' relationships, every depositor can withdraw in full on any day, and there is no penalty for doing it first.

法律依据The authority
  • ABA 模范规则 5.6(a)；纽约州执业行为规则 5.6(a)(1)。除退休福利外，任何合伙协议或雇佣协议都不得限制律师离职后的执业权。各州均有对应条款。
  • Cohen v. Lord, Day & Lord（纽约，1989）与 Denburg v. Parker Chapin（纽约，1993）。纽约州最高法院否定了「竞业即没收」：律所不得因离职者继续执业而扣减其应得款项。
  • 纽约市律协 2025-3 号正式意见（2025 年 8 月）堵上了后门：可没收的奖金、递延薪酬、贷款豁免、资本金扣减，只要起到阻碍竞业的作用，同样不被允许。Kirkland 的递延之所以成立，是因为它是时间安排，不是没收。
  • ABA 489 号正式意见（2019）。通知期不得被用来拖延离职，或阻止客户跟随律师。
  • ABA Model Rule 5.6(a); N.Y. RPC 5.6(a)(1). No partnership or employment agreement may restrict a lawyer's right to practise after leaving, retirement benefits aside. Every state has a version.
  • Cohen v. Lord, Day & Lord (N.Y. 1989) and Denburg v. Parker Chapin (N.Y. 1993). New York's highest court struck down forfeiture-for-competition: a firm cannot dock a departing partner's payout for competing.
  • NYC Bar Formal Op. 2025-3 (Aug 2025) closed the back doors. Forfeitable bonuses, deferred compensation, loan forgiveness and capital deductions that deter competition are impermissible too. Kirkland's deferral survives because it is a timing device, not a forfeiture.
  • ABA Formal Op. 489 (2019). Notice periods cannot be used to delay a departure or keep clients from following the lawyer.

这条规则保护的是客户选择律师的自由，不是律所在律师身上的投入。The rule exists to protect the client's choice of counsel, not the firm's investment in the lawyer.

第四幕 · 治理Act IV · Governance

钱是燃料，领导层是那颗火星 Money was the fuel, leadership was the spark

前合伙人形容这是一家「由个人组成的所」：客户关系属于某几个人，不属于机构。三件事把薪酬差距变成了解散。

Former partners described a firm of individuals: the client relationships belonged to personalities rather than to the institution. Three facts turned a pay gap into a dissolution.

  • 双人轴心。决策都走 Aiello 和 Wolf 这条线。据报管理委员会成员要推动增长方案得先拿到 Aiello 的首肯，至少有一人在一次正面冲突后申请退出委员会。
  • 业务结构押错了边。在 Kirkland、Latham、Simpson 搭建起如今承载交易流的基金发起人平台的那些年里，所里把资源给了上市公司并购而不是私募股权。Weil 的 PE 业务从未做到与它的名声相称。
  • 蒸发掉的接班计划。Nee 三月被宣布为接班人，八月告诉管理层她不会接任。合伙人们在最需要备选方案的时候，发现没有备选方案。
  • An axis of two. Decisions ran through Aiello and Wolf. Management-committee members reportedly needed Aiello's blessing to advance a growth idea; at least one asked to leave the committee after a confrontation.
  • The wrong bet on practice mix. Public M&A was favoured over private equity through the years in which Kirkland, Latham and Simpson built the sponsor platforms that now carry the deal flow. Weil's PE practice never scaled to its own reputation.
  • A succession that evaporated. Nee was named successor in March and told leadership in August she would not serve. The partnership discovered there was no plan B at the moment it needed one.
结构位置Structural position

大到做不成 Cravath 或 Wachtell 那样的专精所，又小到做不成 Kirkland 或 Latham 那样的平台。精英市场的中间地带，正是增长停滞、明星被别人用价格挖走的地方。

Too big to be a specialist like Cravath or Wachtell. Too small to be a platform like Kirkland or Latham. The middle of the elite market is where growth stalls and stars get priced away.

这个剧本演过很多遍The script

Howrey。Dewey & LeBoeuf。Bingham McCutchen。Stroock。团队出走、合并失败，然后表决。Weil 只是照着演的所里最大、最赚钱的那一家。

Howrey. Dewey & LeBoeuf. Bingham McCutchen. Stroock. Group exits, a failed combination, then a vote. Weil is the largest and most profitable firm to follow it.

律所唯一的资产，每天晚上都会走出大楼。 A law firm's only assets walk out of the building every night.

Weil 的合伙人一个接一个地决定，第二天不再走回来。文化、薪酬公式、领导层，都只是「想留下」的理由。理由用完了，法律不会给律所留下任何可以留住人的东西。

Weil's partners decided, one by one, not to walk back in. Culture, pay formula and leadership are only reasons to want to. When the reasons run out, the law offers a firm nothing to hold anyone with.
