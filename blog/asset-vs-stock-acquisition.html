资产收购 vs 股权收购：并购结构的关键抉择

Asset vs. Stock Acquisition: The Critical M&A Structural Choice

8大维度深度对比 · 税务 · 负债 · 合同 · 员工 · 知识产权 · 监管 8 Dimensions · Tax · Liability · Contracts · Employees · IP · Regulatory

文章摘要Summary
在美国并购交易中，选择资产收购（Asset Acquisition）还是股权收购（Stock Acquisition）是决定交易结构的核心问题。两种结构在税务处理、负债隔离、合同转让、员工安排、知识产权过户及监管审批等方面存在根本性差异。买方通常偏向资产收购以获得税基提升和负债隔离；卖方则倾向股权收购以享受更低的资本利得税率。本文系统梳理8大对比维度，并附综合对比表与场景选择指南。 In U.S. M&A transactions, choosing between an asset acquisition and a stock acquisition is one of the most consequential structural decisions. The two approaches differ fundamentally across tax treatment, liability exposure, contract continuity, employee handling, IP transfer, and regulatory approvals. Buyers typically prefer asset deals for step-up basis and liability isolation; sellers prefer stock deals for capital gains treatment. This guide covers all eight key dimensions, with a comparative table and scenario decision framework.

01 · 法律结构01 · Legal Structure

两种结构的本质区别

The Fundamental Difference Between the Two Structures

买什么、卖什么——这一选择决定后续所有条款 What is being bought and sold — this choice shapes every downstream term

资产收购与股权收购的根本区别在于：交易标的是公司的特定资产，还是公司的所有权本身。这一选择对买卖双方在税务、风险、运营连续性等方面产生截然不同的影响。

The fundamental distinction between an asset acquisition and a stock acquisition is: are you buying specific assets of the business, or ownership of the company itself? This choice produces dramatically different consequences for both parties across tax, risk, and operational continuity.

资产收购 · Asset AcquisitionAsset Acquisition
购买特定资产Buying Specific Assets
  • 买方选择性购买特定资产（有形/无形）
  • Buyer selectively acquires specific assets (tangible/intangible)
  • 卖方公司实体在交割后继续存在
  • Seller's legal entity continues to exist post-closing
  • 需逐项列明转让资产清单（Asset Schedule）
  • Requires detailed asset schedule listing each transferred item
  • 买方通常创建新实体或并入现有实体
  • Buyer typically acquires through a new or existing entity

法律边界清晰，买方仅获得已明确约定的资产和负债。

Clear legal boundaries — buyer only acquires expressly agreed assets and assumed liabilities.

股权收购 · Stock AcquisitionStock Acquisition
购买公司所有权Buying Company Ownership
  • 买方购买目标公司股份或成员权益
  • Buyer acquires shares or membership interests of the target
  • 目标公司作为独立实体继续运营，所有权换人
  • Target continues as a legal entity — only ownership changes
  • 公司名称、资质、许可证随实体自动延续
  • Company name, credentials, and licenses continue automatically
  • 卖方退出公司所有权结构，收取对价
  • Sellers exit the ownership structure in exchange for consideration

买方继承整个公司，包括所有已知和未知的风险敞口。

Buyer inherits the entire company — including all known and unknown liabilities.

02 · 税务影响02 · Tax Implications

税务处理：买卖双方的利益博弈

Tax Treatment: The Fundamental Buyer-Seller Tension

IRC §338(h)(10)、§1060 与 stepped-up 税基 IRC §338(h)(10), §1060, and the step-up in basis

税务处理是资产收购与股权收购之间最核心的分歧点。买方通常偏向资产收购（获得 stepped-up 税基，未来折旧/摊销抵税），卖方通常偏向股权收购（只缴资本利得税，税率更低）。这一结构性矛盾是并购谈判中的永恒张力。

Tax treatment is the single biggest point of divergence between the two structures. Buyers typically prefer asset deals (step-up in basis enables future depreciation/amortization deductions); sellers typically prefer stock deals (capital gains treatment at lower rates). This structural tension is a constant feature of M&A negotiations.

买方有利 · Buyer-FavorableBuyer-Favorable
资产收购税务Asset Deal Tax Treatment
  • 买方获得资产「stepped-up」税基（按收购价重新计算）
  • Buyer receives a "stepped-up" tax basis in acquired assets (reset to purchase price)
  • 有形资产可按 MACRS 折旧，无形资产（含商誉）按 IRC §1060 / §197 分配并摊销15年
  • Tangible assets depreciated under MACRS; intangibles (including goodwill) allocated under IRC §1060 and amortized over 15 years per §197
  • 卖方面临两层税：普通收入税（存货、应收款等）+ 资本利得税（资产增值部分）
  • Seller faces two-layer taxation: ordinary income tax (inventory, receivables) + capital gains tax (asset appreciation)
  • C 型公司卖方可能面临"双重征税"（公司层+股东层）
  • C-corp sellers may face "double taxation" (corporate level + shareholder level)
卖方有利 · Seller-FavorableSeller-Favorable
股权收购税务Stock Deal Tax Treatment
  • 卖方通常仅缴长期资本利得税（联邦最高 20%），税率显著低于普通收入税
  • Sellers generally pay long-term capital gains tax (federal max 20%), significantly lower than ordinary income rates
  • 买方无法获得 stepped-up 税基，延续卖方历史税基
  • Buyer receives no step-up — carries over seller's historical (carryover) basis
  • 目标公司的净亏损结转（NOL）随实体转移至买方，但受 IRC §382 限制
  • Target's net operating loss carryforwards (NOL) transfer to buyer, subject to IRC §382 annual limitations
  • 其他税务属性（税收抵免、资本损失结转）也随实体转移
  • Other tax attributes (tax credits, capital loss carryforwards) also transfer with the entity
💡 IRC §338(h)(10) 选举：双方均可接受的妥协方案IRC §338(h)(10) Election: A Mutually Acceptable Compromise

对于 S 型公司或子公司股权收购，双方可以联合选择 IRC §338(h)(10)，将股权交易在税务上视同资产交易处理。买方因此获得 stepped-up 税基；卖方通常可通过谈判要求更高的收购价格作为补偿。这是并购谈判中弥合税务分歧的常见工具。

For acquisitions of S-corporation or subsidiary stock, the parties may jointly elect IRC §338(h)(10) to treat the stock deal as an asset deal for tax purposes. Buyers get the step-up; sellers typically negotiate a higher gross purchase price to compensate for increased tax exposure. This is one of the most common tools for bridging the tax gap in M&A negotiations.

03 · 负债承继03 · Liability Succession

买方的风险隔离与全面承继

Liability Isolation vs. Full Succession

历史负债、诉讼风险与环境责任的分配 Historical liabilities, litigation risk, and environmental responsibility

负债处理是资产收购最大的优势之一。原则上，在资产收购中，买方仅承继交易文件中明确约定的负债；而在股权收购中，买方继承目标公司的全部负债——无论是否已知、是否已披露。

Liability treatment is one of the biggest advantages of an asset deal. In principle, an asset buyer assumes only the liabilities expressly agreed upon in the transaction documents; a stock buyer inherits all of the target's liabilities — whether known, unknown, or undisclosed.

买方有利 · 较好隔离Buyer-Favorable · Better Isolation
资产收购负债处理Asset Deal Liability Treatment
  • 原则上仅承继交易文件中明确约定的负债
  • Generally assumes only liabilities expressly stated in transaction documents
  • 历史诉讼风险留存于卖方实体
  • Historical litigation risk remains with seller's entity
  • 环境污染责任可有效隔离（CERCLA 例外须注意）
  • Environmental liabilities can be effectively isolated (watch CERCLA successor liability exceptions)
  • 税务审计风险不随资产转移
  • Tax audit risk does not follow the assets

⚠️ 注意：产品责任、劳工法下的"继承者责任"理论可能突破隔离。

⚠️ Note: "Successor liability" doctrines in products liability and labor law may pierce the isolation.

风险较高 · 全面承继Higher Risk · Full Succession
股权收购负债处理Stock Deal Liability Treatment
  • 买方承继目标公司全部已知及未知负债
  • Buyer inherits all known and unknown liabilities of the target
  • 包括未决诉讼、潜在环境责任、税务敞口
  • Including pending litigation, environmental exposure, and tax liabilities
  • 历史税务审计风险随公司实体转移至买方
  • Historical tax audit risk transfers to buyer with the company entity
  • 需通过全面尽职调查量化并定价未知风险
  • Requires comprehensive due diligence to identify, quantify, and price unknown risks

陈述与保证保险（R&W Insurance）是股权收购中核心风控工具。

Representations & Warranties Insurance (R&W) is a core risk management tool in stock deals.

04 · 合同转让04 · Contract Assignment

客户合同与许可证：逐一取得同意还是自动延续？

Customer Contracts and Licenses: Consent Required or Automatic Continuity?

变更控制条款与合同存续风险 Change-of-control clauses and contract continuity risk

合同转让的处理方式是资产收购与股权收购之间最直接影响交易执行效率的差异。在资产收购中，每一份重要合同原则上需要对方当事人的书面同意才能转让；而股权收购中，合同主体（目标公司）未变，理论上无需重新取得同意。

Contract assignment treatment is one of the most directly impactful differences on deal execution efficiency. In an asset deal, each material contract in principle requires counterparty consent to assign; in a stock deal, the contracting party (the target company) is unchanged — so consent is theoretically not required.

繁琐 · 逐一取得同意Burdensome · Consent Required
资产收购合同处理Asset Deal Contract Handling
  • 每份重要合同须单独取得对方书面同意
  • Each material contract requires separate written counterparty consent
  • 含"变更控制条款"的合同须重新谈判或获批准
  • Contracts with "change of control" provisions must be renegotiated or waived
  • 客户合同转让存在流失风险（对方借机重谈或终止）
  • Customer contract assignment carries attrition risk (counterparty may renegotiate or terminate)
  • 政府合同通常需要监管机构特别审批
  • Government contracts typically require separate regulatory approval
便利 · 通常自动延续Convenient · Generally Automatic
股权收购合同处理Stock Deal Contract Handling
  • 合同主体未变，理论上无需重新取得对方同意
  • Contracting party unchanged — consent theoretically not required
  • 许可证、行业资质通常随实体继续有效
  • Licenses and industry qualifications generally remain valid with the entity
  • 但仍需审查是否有明确的"股权变更触发条款"
  • Must still review for explicit "equity change of control" trigger provisions
  • PE/VC 支持企业的合同常含股权变更触发条款
  • PE/VC-backed companies often have equity change-of-control triggers in key contracts
⚠️ 变更控制条款是最容易被忽视的风险点Change-of-Control Clauses: The Most Overlooked Risk

无论是资产收购还是股权收购，对目标公司所有重要合同（客户合同、供应商协议、许可证、融资协议、关键员工协议）进行系统性"变更控制条款扫描"是尽职调查中不可省略的步骤。遗漏一份关键合同的变更控制条款，可能在交割后立即引发重大业务中断。

Regardless of deal structure, a systematic "change-of-control clause sweep" across all material contracts (customer agreements, supplier contracts, licenses, financing arrangements, key employee agreements) is a non-negotiable due diligence step. Missing a single change-of-control provision in a critical contract can trigger a major business disruption immediately post-closing.

05 · 员工处理05 · Employee Treatment

选择性录用还是全员随实体转移？

Selective Hiring vs. Automatic Transfer of All Employees?

WARN Act、CBA 与员工留存风险 WARN Act, CBAs, and employee retention risk

员工安排的差异直接影响交割后的人才留存和劳工合规风险。资产收购给买方提供了选择性录用员工的灵活性；股权收购则意味着全体员工随公司整体转移，现有雇用条款延续。

Employee treatment differences directly impact post-closing talent retention and labor compliance risk. An asset deal gives the buyer flexibility to selectively hire employees; a stock deal means all employees transfer with the company, with existing employment terms continuing.

灵活 · 可选择性录用Flexible · Selective Hiring
资产收购员工处理Asset Deal Employee Handling
  • 买方可选择性录用员工，无需全员接收
  • Buyer may selectively hire employees — not required to offer employment to all
  • 原雇用关系在交割时终止，需重新签订劳动合同
  • Existing employment relationships terminate at closing; new agreements must be executed
  • 若涉及大规模裁员，可能触发 WARN Act（提前60天通知义务）
  • Mass layoffs may trigger WARN Act (60-day advance notice requirement)
  • 福利计划（401k、健康险）需重新设立或衔接，存在"过渡期"风险
  • Benefit plans (401k, health insurance) must be re-established; "gap period" risk exists

可精准选择核心人才，但需防范歧视性录用（拒绝受保护群体）的法律风险。

Can target key talent precisely, but must guard against discriminatory hiring (rejecting protected classes).

稳定 · 全员随实体转移Stable · All Employees Transfer
股权收购员工处理Stock Deal Employee Handling
  • 全体员工随公司整体转移，原有雇用合同延续
  • All employees transfer with the company; existing employment contracts continue
  • 福利计划（401k、健康险、股权激励）延续有效
  • Benefit plans (401k, health insurance, equity incentives) remain in effect
  • 工会协议（CBA）随实体继续有效，买方须遵守
  • Collective bargaining agreements (CBAs) continue with the entity; buyer must comply
  • 交割触发员工留任激励（Retention Bonus）通常在此时支付
  • Closing often triggers retention bonuses; timing and payment mechanics need attention
06 · 知识产权转让06 · IP Transfer

专利、商标与域名：逐一过户还是自动延续？

Patents, Trademarks & Domains: Individual Transfer or Automatic Continuity?

USPTO 登记、IP 链条审查与第三方许可 USPTO registration, IP chain of title, and third-party licenses

知识产权（IP）是科技、品牌和创意行业并购的核心标的。资产收购需要逐一完成知识产权的形式转让手续；股权收购则因公司主体不变，IP 随实体自动延续，但须确认 IP 确实归属目标公司所有。

Intellectual property is the core acquisition target in technology, brand, and creative industry deals. Asset deals require formal transfer procedures for each IP asset; stock deals allow IP to continue automatically with the entity — but confirming the target actually owns the IP remains critical.

需逐一登记过户Individual Registration Required
资产收购 IP 处理Asset Deal IP Handling
  • 专利须向 USPTO 提交转让登记（Assignment of Patent）
  • Patents require USPTO Assignment recordation
  • 商标须向 USPTO 提交商标转让申请（Assignment of Trademark）
  • Trademarks require USPTO Trademark Assignment filing
  • 版权转让需单独书面协议，并可向版权局登记
  • Copyright transfers require a separate written agreement; optional Copyright Office recordation
  • 域名、社交媒体账号、商业秘密需逐一过户和交接
  • Domain names, social media accounts, and trade secrets require individual transfer and handoff

IP 链条（Chain of Title）审查是科技行业资产收购尽调的核心项目。

IP chain-of-title review is a core diligence item in tech asset acquisitions.

随实体自动延续Continues Automatically with Entity
股权收购 IP 处理Stock Deal IP Handling
  • IP 所有权随公司实体自动延续，无需单独过户
  • IP ownership continues automatically with the entity — no separate transfer needed
  • 仍需确认 IP 确实属于目标公司所有（非创始人个人）
  • Must confirm IP is actually owned by the target (not founder personally)
  • 审查第三方许可协议中的"控制权变更"终止条款
  • Review third-party license agreements for "change of control" termination triggers
  • 确认开源软件使用合规，避免 Copyleft 风险
  • Verify open-source software compliance; avoid copyleft contamination risk
07 · 监管审批07 · Regulatory Approvals

HSR 申报、CFIUS 与行业特许审批

HSR Filing, CFIUS, and Industry-Specific Approvals

反垄断申报阈值与外资审查差异 Antitrust filing thresholds and foreign investment review differences

两种结构在监管审批方面的主要差异体现在：HSR 申报阈值的计算基础不同、行业许可证的处理方式不同，以及 CFIUS 对外资股权收购的审查通常更为严格。

The key regulatory differences relate to: how HSR filing thresholds are calculated, how industry licenses are handled, and the generally stricter CFIUS scrutiny applied to foreign stock acquisitions.

视行业 · 许可证须重新申请Industry-Dependent · Licenses Require Refiling
资产收购监管处理Asset Deal Regulatory Handling
  • HSR 申报阈值以被转让资产的公允市场价值计算
  • HSR filing threshold calculated based on fair market value of transferred assets
  • 行业许可证（金融、医疗、电信等）通常需向监管机构重新申请
  • Industry licenses (financial, healthcare, telecom) typically must be re-applied for
  • CFIUS 审查视具体转让资产的性质和战略重要性而定
  • CFIUS review depends on nature and strategic importance of specific transferred assets
  • 环境许可证转让可能需要州环保机构单独审批
  • Environmental permits may require separate state agency approval for transfer
视规模 · 许可证通常延续Scale-Dependent · Licenses Generally Continue
股权收购监管处理Stock Deal Regulatory Handling
  • HSR 申报阈值以收购对价（股权价值）计算
  • HSR filing threshold based on acquisition consideration (equity value)
  • 现有许可证通常随实体继续有效，无需重新申请
  • Existing licenses generally remain valid with the entity — no refiling required
  • CFIUS 对外资股权收购通常审查更为严格（特别是涉及关键技术或基础设施）
  • CFIUS typically applies stricter scrutiny to foreign stock acquisitions (especially critical tech or infrastructure)
  • 银行、保险、电信等特许行业需专项监管审批，审批周期长
  • Banking, insurance, and telecom require specific regulatory approvals with long review timelines
08 · 交易执行复杂度08 · Transaction Complexity

APA vs SPA：文件结构与交割复杂度

APA vs. SPA: Document Structure and Closing Complexity

资产购买协议 vs 股权购买协议的核心差异 The core differences between an Asset Purchase Agreement and a Stock Purchase Agreement

从交易执行角度，资产收购通常需要更多的交割文件（每类资产需单独的转让文件），但股权收购的尽职调查范围通常更广（需审查整个公司）。

From an execution perspective, asset deals typically require more closing documents (each asset category needs a separate transfer instrument), while stock deals generally involve broader due diligence scope (reviewing the entire company).

文件多 · 交割清单长More Documents · Longer Closing Checklist
资产购买协议（APA）Asset Purchase Agreement (APA)
  • 核心协议：资产购买协议（APA），附件包括资产清单、承继负债清单
  • Core document: Asset Purchase Agreement (APA) with schedules listing acquired assets and assumed liabilities
  • 须逐项起草资产转让文件（专利让渡书、商标转让书、不动产过户契约等）
  • Individual transfer instruments required for each asset class (patent assignments, trademark assignments, deeds, etc.)
  • 交割清单通常较长，各类资产过户手续并行推进
  • Closing checklist typically longer; multiple asset transfer procedures running in parallel
  • 不动产转让须额外办理产权登记，可能延长交割时间线
  • Real estate transfer requires title recordation, potentially extending closing timeline
文件少 · 尽调范围广Fewer Documents · Broader Diligence Scope
股权购买协议（SPA）Stock Purchase Agreement (SPA)
  • 核心协议：股权购买协议（SPA），附件包括股权陈述保证清单
  • Core document: Stock Purchase Agreement (SPA) with disclosure schedules
  • 股权/成员权益转让手续相对简单（股权转让书或 LLC Interest Assignment）
  • Equity/membership interest transfer is relatively simple (stock transfer certificate or LLC Interest Assignment)
  • 交割清单聚焦于股权交割、董事会决议和监管批准文件
  • Closing checklist focuses on equity delivery, board resolutions, and regulatory approval documentation
  • 但尽职调查范围更广——需审查整个公司的历史运营和全部负债
  • But due diligence scope is broader — reviewing the entire company's historical operations and all liabilities
"在并购谈判中，买方代理律师的第一选择几乎总是资产收购；卖方代理律师的第一选择几乎总是股权收购。最终交易结构，是双方律师和财务顾问在税务、风险和运营便利性之间博弈的结果。" "In M&A negotiations, the buyer's counsel almost always prefers an asset deal; seller's counsel almost always prefers a stock deal. The final structure is the product of negotiation between both sides' lawyers and financial advisors, balancing tax, risk, and operational convenience."

哪种结构更适合你的交易？

Which Structure Is Right for Your Deal?

基于交易特征的选择框架 A selection framework based on deal characteristics

没有普遍适用的"最优结构"——选择取决于双方的税务状况、风险偏好、目标公司特征和行业监管环境。以下是常见场景下的参考框架。

There is no universally "optimal" structure — the choice depends on each party's tax position, risk tolerance, target company characteristics, and regulatory environment. Below is a reference framework for common scenarios.

📋
优先考虑资产收购的场景
When to Prefer an Asset Deal
  • 目标公司存在重大未知或未披露负债
  • 买方希望最大化未来税务折旧/摊销抵扣
  • 仅需要目标公司的特定业务线或资产
  • 目标公司有大量诉讼或环境污染风险
  • 买方税率较高，stepped-up 税基价值显著
  • Target has significant unknown or undisclosed liabilities
  • Buyer wants to maximize future depreciation/amortization deductions
  • Only a specific business line or asset set is needed
  • Target carries substantial litigation or environmental exposure
  • Buyer has a high effective tax rate making step-up especially valuable
🏢
优先考虑股权收购的场景
When to Prefer a Stock Deal
  • 目标公司拥有大量难以转让或不可转让的合同
  • 行业许可证/资质重新申请成本或周期极高
  • 卖方坚持资本利得税处理（税率优惠）
  • 目标公司具有 NOL 等有价值的税务属性
  • 员工稳定性对业务连续性至关重要
  • Target has many contracts that are non-assignable or difficult to transfer
  • Re-obtaining industry licenses would be prohibitively expensive or time-consuming
  • Seller insists on capital gains treatment (favorable tax rates)
  • Target has valuable tax attributes such as NOL carryforwards
  • Employee stability is critical for business continuity post-closing
💡 实践中的混合方案Hybrid Solutions in Practice

在实践中，许多并购交易会综合运用两种结构的优势：例如通过 IRC §338(h)(10) 选举使股权交易享有资产交易的税务待遇；或采用合并（Merger）结构替代直接资产/股权收购，在特定情形下既实现负债隔离又保持合同延续。选择最优结构需要并购律师与税务顾问的密切协作，结合目标公司的具体情况综合分析。

In practice, many M&A transactions combine elements of both structures: for example, a §338(h)(10) election gives a stock deal asset-deal tax treatment; or a merger structure may achieve both liability isolation and contract continuity in certain scenarios. Selecting the optimal structure requires close collaboration between M&A counsel and tax advisors, tailored to the specific facts of the target company.
