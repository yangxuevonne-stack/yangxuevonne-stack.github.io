01 · 源起与本质01 · Origins & Essence

什么是 QSBSWhat is QSBS

Section 1202 — A Tax Code Gift Most Founders Don't Fully Understand

QSBS——Qualified Small Business Stock，合格小企业股票——是美国《国内税收法典》第1202条（IRC § 1202）下的一项税收激励。它的核心承诺极其简单：如果你持有满足条件的小企业股票超过规定年限，出售时，可以将巨额资本利得排除在联邦所得税之外。

这条法律1993年由克林顿政府签署成法，最初的目的是把资本引导到中小企业。但在硅谷崛起后，QSBS的真正受益者变成了科技初创公司的创始人、早期员工和天使投资人。一笔成功的IPO或并购退出，配合正确的QSBS规划，可以让一位创始人合法地把数千万乃至上亿美元的资本利得变成完全免税的现金。

2025年7月4日，特朗普签署的"One Big Beautiful Bill Act"（OBBBA，《大而美法案》）是QSBS立法十多年来最重要的一次升级。它做了三件事：把单一发行人免税额度从1000万美元提高到1500万、引入分级持有期（3年/4年/5年=50%/75%/100%免税）、并把"小企业"的总资产门槛从5000万提高到7500万。

这意味着，即使你的初创公司估值已经达到7500万美元的临界点，新发行的股份依然可以是QSBS。也意味着，如果你在第3年遇到收购机会，不再需要为了等满5年而强行延迟交易——可以拿走50%的免税额度先走再说。

QSBS — Qualified Small Business Stock — is the tax incentive under Internal Revenue Code Section 1202. Its promise is simple: hold qualifying small business stock for the required period, and when you sell, a massive chunk of the capital gain can be excluded from federal income tax entirely.

Enacted in 1993 to channel capital toward small businesses, Section 1202 became, in the Silicon Valley era, the secret weapon of startup founders, early employees, and angel investors. A successful IPO or acquisition exit, paired with proper QSBS planning, can convert tens of millions — sometimes hundreds of millions — of capital gain into fully tax-free cash.

On July 4, 2025, President Trump signed the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA), the most important QSBS upgrade in over a decade. It did three things: raised the per-issuer exclusion from $10M to $15M, introduced tiered holding periods (3/4/5 years = 50%/75%/100% exclusion), and bumped the corporate gross asset cap from $50M to $75M.

Translation: companies that previously aged out of QSBS at $50M can now keep issuing qualified shares up to $75M. And if a buyer shows up at year three, you no longer have to choose between a deal and the tax shield — you can take 50% of the benefit and walk.

02 · 资格要件02 · Qualification Requirements

三道必须同时通过的关卡Three Gates You Must Pass Simultaneously

Company Test · Shareholder Test · Holding Period

QSBS的吸引力如此之大，以至于税务局对它的资格审查异常严格。任何一道关卡失败，整笔免税待遇都会作废。

第一关：公司层面

QSBS's generosity is matched by the IRS's strictness. Miss any one gate, and the entire exclusion vanishes.

Gate One: The Company

①合格小企业（Qualified Small Business）Qualified Small Business Test
  • 美国C型公司：必须是美国国内的C corporation。LLC、S corporation、合伙企业都不符合。LLC转C-corp的转换日是新的"原始发行日"。U.S. domestic C corporation: LLCs, S corps, and partnerships do not qualify. The date of an LLC-to-C-corp conversion becomes the new "original issuance" date.
  • 总资产门槛：在你的股票发行时（以及发行后立即），公司总资产（按税基计算）不得超过$7500万（OBBBA后；2025年7月4日之前发行的股票仍是$5000万门槛）。Gross asset ceiling: At and immediately after issuance, total gross assets must not exceed $75M (post-OBBBA; pre-7/4/25 stock retains the $50M cap).
  • 积极业务测试：公司至少80%的资产必须用于"合格行业"——基本上排除了金融、保险、农业、矿业、酒店、餐饮，以及大多数"个人服务行业"（律所、医生、咨询、表演、运动等）。Active business test: 80%+ of assets must be used in a "qualified trade or business." Excludes finance, insurance, farming, mining, hotels, restaurants, and most personal service industries.
  • 原始发行：股票必须直接从公司获得，作为换取现金、财产或服务的对价。从其他股东手上买的二手股不符合（赠与是关键例外）。Original issuance: Stock must come directly from the company in exchange for cash, property, or services. Secondary purchases don't qualify — gifts are the critical exception.

第二关：股东层面Gate Two: The Shareholder

②合格股东Eligible Shareholder Test
  • 非C型公司纳税人：个人、合伙企业、S corporation、信托——都可以。但持股的C corporation不行。Non-corporate taxpayer: Individuals, partnerships, S corps, and trusts qualify. C corporations as shareholders do not.
  • 赠与例外：第1202(h)条规定，通过赠与、遗产继承获得QSBS的人，"踏入前手鞋子"——继承前手的持有期和成本基础，并享有自己独立的免税额度。这一条是stacking策略的法律基石。The gift exception: Under § 1202(h), recipients via gift or inheritance "step into the shoes" — inheriting holding period and basis, while qualifying for their own independent exclusion. This is the legal foundation of the entire stacking strategy.

04 · 免税额度数学04 · The Exclusion Math

$1500万 vs 10倍基础，谁更划算？$15M vs 10× Basis — Which Wins?

The Per-Issuer Cap Has Two Limbs

第1202条的免税上限是"取较大值"：

最大免税额 = MAX( $15,000,000 , 10 × 你的成本基础 )

对于成本基础极低的创始人——比如从零开始用1美元换100万股——10×基础几乎没有意义。所以创始人的实际上限就是$1500万。

但对于后续大额投资人——比如以$5000万买入Series B股份的VC——10×基础=$5亿，远远超过$1500万。这就是为什么VC的QSBS税务规划逻辑和创始人完全不同：他们追求的是"放大基础"。

Section 1202's per-issuer cap is the greater of two numbers:

Max Exclusion = MAX( $15,000,000 , 10 × your basis )

For founders with near-zero basis (think: $1 for 1M shares), 10× basis is effectively meaningless. The hard cap is $15M.

But for a later-stage investor — say a VC putting $50M into Series B — 10× basis = $500M, far above $15M. VCs amplify basis, founders multiply taxpayers.

两个关键变量Two Critical Variables

关键变量 1Variable 1

每个发行人独立计算Per-Issuer, Not Per-Transaction

免税额度是"per issuer"——每家发行公司独立有一个$1500万。如果你同时持有公司A和公司B的QSBS，可以分别用满两次$1500万。The cap is "per issuer" — each company gives you a fresh $15M. Hold QSBS in both Company A and Company B? You get $15M for each.

关键变量 2Variable 2

每个纳税人独立计算Per-Taxpayer, Not Per-Family

"每个纳税人"是stacking的命门。只要每一位持有人都是法律上独立的纳税主体，他们各自享有完整的$1500万额度。"Per taxpayer" is the keystone of stacking. As long as each holder is a separate tax entity, each gets a full $15M.

05 · Stacking 核心原理05 · Stacking — Core Mechanics

把1500万变成1.5亿的合法路径Turning $15M into $150M — Legally

The Strategy That Compounds the Per-Taxpayer Cap

Stacking——直译"叠加"——是创始人税务规划中威力最大的单一技术。它的逻辑可以一句话总结：

QSBS免税额度是"每个纳税人 × 每个发行人"。
如果你能合法地创造更多纳税人，你就能合法地累加更多$1500万。The QSBS exclusion is "per taxpayer × per issuer." Create more legitimate taxpayers — and the $15M caps multiply.

核心机制依赖于第1202(h)条的赠与条款。当你把QSBS在签订有约束力的销售协议之前，通过赠与方式转给：你的成年子女、父母、兄弟姐妹、独立设立的非授予人信托——受赠方继承你的持有期和成本基础，但获得他/她/它自己独立的$1500万免税额度。

Stacking is the single most powerful technique in founder tax planning. The mechanic in one sentence:

The QSBS cap is "per taxpayer × per issuer."
Legitimately create more taxpayers, and you legitimately stack more $15M caps.Compound the cap by compounding the taxpayers — without breaking a single rule.

配偶规则的细节A Note on the Spouse Rule

配偶并不能简单地把$1500万翻倍。第1202条对联合申报的处理是把单一$1500万额度在夫妻间分配，而非自动翻倍。要让配偶贡献额度，通常需要通过SLANT（配偶终身受益权非授予人信托）或更复杂的结构。A spouse does not automatically double the cap. Section 1202 treats the joint-return cap as something allocated between spouses, not doubled. To get the spouse's "slot," planners typically use a SLANT or more elaborate structures.

06 · 信托工具箱06 · The Trust Toolkit

非授予人信托：Stacking引擎Non-Grantor Trusts: The Stacking Engine

Why "Non-Grantor" Is the Word That Multiplies Your Exclusion

Stacking最常用的工具是非授予人信托（Non-Grantor Trust, NGT）。理解它，需要先理解一个反例：

"授予人信托（Grantor Trust）"在所得税上是透明的——信托内的所有收入和资本利得仍然归创建人（the grantor）报税。它不是独立纳税人。所以对于QSBS而言，gift to a grantor trust = no new exclusion. 把股票放进授予人信托，就像把钱从左口袋放到右口袋——没有税务效果。

"非授予人信托"则是独立的纳税主体，它有自己的EIN（雇主识别号）、自己提交1041表格、自己缴所得税——也因此享有自己独立的$1500万QSBS免税额度。每多一个非授予人信托，就多一个$1500万的"槽位"。

The default tool for stacking is the Non-Grantor Trust (NGT). To understand it, start with the foil:

A "grantor trust" is transparent for income tax purposes — all income and gains flow back to the grantor's personal return. It is not a separate taxpayer. So gifting QSBS to a grantor trust = no new exclusion. It's moving cash from your left pocket to your right.

A non-grantor trust is a separate taxable entity — its own EIN, its own Form 1041, its own income tax — and therefore its own independent $15M QSBS cap. Each properly structured NGT is one more $15M slot.

⚠ 州税陷阱⚠ State Tax Trap

联邦层面的QSBS免税并不等于州层面的免税。加州、纽约、宾州、新泽西不承认或仅部分承认QSBS免税——这意味着即使联邦零税，州税可能仍要承担13.3%（加州）的资本利得税。Stacking规划必须考虑信托设立州（situs）：特拉华、内华达、南达科他、华盛顿等是常见首选。Federal QSBS exclusion ≠ state exclusion. California, New York, Pennsylvania, and New Jersey do not fully conform — meaning even with $0 federal tax, you may still owe up to 13.3% (CA) state capital gains tax. Stacking planning must consider trust situs.

08 · 红线与风险08 · Red Lines & Risks

IRS 在哪里会出手Where the IRS Pushes Back

Five Failure Modes That Unwind the Whole Strategy
风险 01Risk 01
"收入归属"原则Assignment-of-Income Doctrine
如果你在已经"实质上拥有买家"的情况下进行赠与，IRS可能裁定这笔利得在赠与之前已经"实现"。规则：所有赠与必须在任何具有约束力的销售协议之前完成。实务上，安全期是LOI之前的12–18个月。The rule: All gifts must occur before any binding sale agreement. If you gift after a buyer is essentially locked in, the IRS can claim the gain was "realized" before the gift. Safe zone: 12–18 months before any LOI.
风险 02Risk 02
IRC § 643(f)：多信托合并规则§ 643(f) Multiple-Trust Anti-Abuse Rule
国会授权IRS"合并"多个信托视为一个：(a) 实质上同一授予人和主要受益人；(b) 主要目的是避税。实务建议：每个信托应有独立受益人、独立条款、独立受托人，2–4个是稳健的，10个为单一受益人设立的信托是激进的。Congress authorized the IRS to "collapse" multiple trusts where (a) substantially the same grantor & primary beneficiary, and (b) a principal purpose is tax avoidance. Practical guidance: Each trust needs distinct beneficiaries, terms, and trustees. Two to four trusts is defensible.
风险 03Risk 03
授予人信托身份意外触发Inadvertent Grantor-Trust Status
如果信托起草不当（保留分配权、对受托人有过多控制权），可能被税法重新归类为"授予人信托"——所有所得归回创建人，丧失独立纳税人身份。整个stacking策略归零。A poorly drafted trust can be reclassified as a grantor trust — income flows back to the grantor, separate taxpayer status is lost. The entire stacking strategy collapses.
风险 04Risk 04
公司层面资格丧失Company-Level Disqualification
公司在持有期内必须持续满足"积极业务"测试。如果公司转向"非合格业务"，或多次回购股份触发反规避规则，QSBS资格可能被追溯取消。The company must continuously satisfy the active-business test through the holding period. Pivots toward non-qualified businesses, or share repurchases triggering anti-abuse rules, can retroactively disqualify QSBS status.

州税不一致State Conformity Risk

Why Stacking Is a 2-Year Project, Not a 2-Month Sprint

Stacking最常见的失败原因，不是规则不懂，而是开始得太晚。当你在LOI前6个月才开始考虑赠与，已经为时已晚——IRS会用assignment of income原则把所有税务效益打回原形。

The most common reason stacking fails isn't a misunderstanding of the rules — it's starting too late. Begin thinking about gifts six months before LOI, and the IRS can use assignment-of-income to unwind everything.

杠杆的代价与回报The Price and Reward of Leverage

When Stacking Makes Sense — and When It Doesn't

QSBS Stacking是合法税务规划中威力最强、起步最难、技术性最高的策略之一。它的真正魅力不在节税本身——而在于它揭示了美国税法的一个根本逻辑：税法激励的是"提前规划者"，不是"事后聪明者"。

但杠杆都有代价。Stacking意味着：放弃控制权（被赠予的股票，无论是给子女还是给信托，你都不再实质拥有。如果家庭关系发生变化或公司退出失败，这些资产无法收回）；动用终身赠与免税额（每笔赠与都消耗你的终身赠与/遗产税免税额，2026年约$1500万/人）；高昂的执行成本（律师费、信托设立费、独立受托人费用、独立估值费、年度1041报税——一套完整的stacking架构，前期投入15万–50万美元是常态。只有在预期免税收益超过200万美元时，这个投入才在经济上合理）。

对真正的合适人群——即将退出的高潜力创始人——这些代价相对于潜在节税额都微不足道。但对于不到$2000万退出预期的创业者，简单地把QSBS用满$1500万就够了，不需要stacking。

真正的智慧在于：知道这件事存在，知道它什么时候开始有用，知道它何时变得过度复杂。这也是为什么早期咨询是关键——不是为了立即执行，而是为了让你在未来某个关键时刻，有正确的选择权。

QSBS Stacking is one of the most powerful, hardest to start, and most technically demanding strategies in legal tax planning. Its real beauty isn't the tax savings — it's what it reveals about the underlying logic of U.S. tax law: the code rewards advance planners, not after-the-fact strategists.

But leverage has a price. Stacking means: loss of control (gifted stock is no longer substantively yours); using lifetime gift exemption (~$15M per person in 2026); significant execution cost ($150K–$500K upfront — economically rational only when expected savings exceed $2M).

For the right candidates — high-potential founders approaching exit — these costs are trivial relative to potential savings. But for entrepreneurs with sub-$20M exit expectations, simply maximizing your single $15M cap is enough; stacking is overkill.

Real wisdom is: knowing this exists, knowing when it starts mattering, and knowing when it becomes overengineered. That's why early consultation matters — not for immediate execution, but to ensure that when the critical moment arrives, you still have the right options on the table.

税法的最大慷慨，从来不是"宽容"，而是"奖励准备"。The tax code's greatest generosity is not leniency — it is rewarding preparation.
