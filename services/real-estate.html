常见风险Common Pitfalls

房产交易中的隐形成本The Hidden Costs of Going Solo

很多中国买家以为买美国房产很简单。实际上，美国房地产体系复杂、风险众多、一步错可能付出数十万美元代价。Many Chinese buyers think buying US real estate is straightforward. The reality: complex regulations, hidden liabilities, and one misstep can cost you six figures.

01
"不就是买个房子吗""It's Just a House Purchase"
中国买家低估美国房地产的法律复杂度。产权问题（title defects）、留置权（liens）、分区限制（zoning restrictions）、HOA 条款、环境污染责任——任何一项都能毁掉交易或导致日后巨大损失。买家经常要么忽视这些风险，要么等到关键时刻才发现问题，时间和金钱都损失了。 Chinese buyers underestimate the legal complexity of US real estate. Title defects, existing liens, zoning violations, HOA covenant traps, environmental liabilities — any one can kill a deal or cost you dearly years down the line. Many buyers either ignore these risks or discover problems at closing, when it's too late and too expensive to back out.
02
FIRPTA 预扣税吓人FIRPTA Withholding Shock
外国投资人卖房产时，美国会自动预扣 15% 的售价作为联邦所得税。很多买家完全没意识到购房时就要规划卖房的税务。正确的持有架构（LLC vs 信托 vs 个人）、FIRPTA 豁免条件、国际税收协定利用——这些可以省下数十万。没有提前规划，你可能多交一倍的税。 Foreign investors face automatic 15% withholding on real estate sale proceeds. Most buyers have zero plan for this when purchasing. The right holding structure (LLC vs. trust vs. personal), FIRPTA exemptions, treaty benefits — these can save six figures at sale. No plan = overpaying by 50% or more.
03
中介说"不需要律师""You Don't Need a Lawyer"
很多美国州没有强制要求聘请律师（特别是住宅购买），地产中介和贷款方经常劝买家省这笔费用。但律师审查购房合同（Purchase Agreement）能发现中介漏掉的关键问题：检查期权条款不完整、贷款条件模糊、过户成本分配不当。最后一刻发现问题，你被迫接受不利条款或者损失定金。 Many US states don't require an attorney for residential purchases, so agents and lenders push buyers to skip legal review and save the fee. But an attorney-reviewed purchase agreement catches what agents miss: incomplete inspection contingencies, vague financing conditions, unfair closing cost allocation. Discovering problems at the 11th hour means accepting bad terms or losing your earnest money deposit.
